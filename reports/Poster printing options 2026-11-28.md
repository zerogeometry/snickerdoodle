# Poster printing options (28 Nov 2026)

**The order:** about 10 A4 and 5 A3 posters (at most 20 A4 and 10 A3) for shop windows, café and school noticeboards during the Christmas push.
**How prices were checked:** every price below was read on Sat 28 Nov 2026 from each printer's own public price grid or quote calculator. Nobody created an account, entered a postcode or started a checkout, and no order was placed. Prices **include VAT and delivery** unless a cell says otherwise.

## The answer

> **Order from [Doxdirect](https://www.doxdirect.com/print-and-bind-online/posters/). Choose 300gsm Satin, no lamination. It costs £13.08 for 10 A4 + 5 A3, or £21.30 for 20 A4 + 10 A3, with free tracked delivery. Order it today and it should arrive by Thu 3 Dec.**

- **Why Doxdirect:** it's the cheapest good option by a long way, well under half the price of the next-best online printer (about £33 for the same small order). It prints from 1 copy, delivery is free from £10, and it's rated 4.8 on Trustpilot from about 35,000 reviews.
- **Why 300gsm Satin:**
  - Satin keeps the crayon colours bright without the glare gloss gets behind shop glass or under café lights.
  - 300gsm is card-weight, so it stays flat in a window, doesn't curl on a corkboard and takes a drawing pin without tearing.
  - It costs only about £1.36 more than their thinner 170gsm satin on the small order.
- **About the date:** Doxdirect makes the posters in 2 days and doesn't count Sundays. Its help page says a Saturday order on standard production goes out on **Tue 1 Dec** by Royal Mail Tracked. Arrival on **Wed 2 or Thu 3 Dec** is my estimate, because they don't publish how long the post takes. If you order on Monday, add a day, so about **Fri 4 Dec**.

### Runner-up: Instantprint, if Doxdirect can't make the date

> **[Instantprint](https://www.instantprint.co.uk/posters), 150gsm Silk.** "Fast" costs **about £34** for 10 A4 + 5 A3 and arrives Mon 7 Dec. "Express" costs **about £44** and arrives **Tue 1 Dec**, as long as the artwork is approved by 5pm on the order day. Its 250gsm silk is about £5 more.

- Rated 4.8 from about 21,000 reviews.
- It prints to the edge and checks the artwork for free.
- Delivery is £4.99. It isn't clear whether one delivery charge covers both sizes, so check the basket.
- For 20 A4, buy 25. Instantprint's price for 25 is lower than its price for 20.

### If you need them today

[Ryman's print service](https://www.ryman.co.uk/print-your-document) does colour prints on 170gsm silk for:
- 95p each for A4 and £1.82 each for A3, plus £2.50 per job
- collected from a Ryman shop in about an hour, at 180+ shops

That comes to about **£21 for 10 A4 + 5 A3**. The catch is that a document printer leaves a thin **white edge**: the cream background won't go to the edge of the paper. Good for a quick top-up, not the main run.

## Which paper for bright crayon colours indoors

| Finish | Good for us? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Satin / silk, 170–300gsm** | ✅ Best | Colours stay bright and rich, and there's little glare behind glass or under lights. 250–300gsm stays flat and feels like a proper poster. |
| Gloss | ⚠️ Okay | The brightest colours, but it bounces light off shop windows and shiny noticeboards, so it's harder to read from an angle. It also shows fingerprints. |
| Matt / uncoated | ⚠️ Meh | No glare, but uncoated paper soaks up ink, so tomato and cobalt look flatter and duller. Vistaprint's "matte" is coated, which is fine, but its 130gsm default is thin. |
| Photo paper, 260gsm | ✅ Lovely, but pricey | The best colour. At £9–£15 a sheet, only worth it for one "hero" poster. |
| Lamination | ❌ Skip | Indoors it isn't needed and it multiplies the price. A laminated A3 ×5 at Doxdirect is about £40. |

Under 130gsm, posters ripple when taped inside a window and tear on drawing pins. Aim for **170gsm or heavier**.

## Exact file spec for Doxdirect

**Your turn** when you export the posters:

| | **A4 poster** | **A3 poster** |
|---|---|---|
| Finished (trim) size | 210 × 297 mm | 297 × 420 mm |
| **Document size, including 3 mm bleed on every edge** | **216 × 303 mm** | **303 × 426 mm** |
| Pixels at 300 dpi, including bleed | **2552 × 3579 px** | **3579 × 5032 px** |
| Keep words, logo and QR code at least | 6 mm inside the trim | 6 mm inside the trim |

- **File type:** PDF. Doxdirect also takes PNG or JPEG, but PDF keeps the text sharp. Make **one single-page PDF per poster size**: Doxdirect prints one side, one page per file.
- **Bleed:** run the cream background and any colour that touches an edge **right out to the edge of the bleed**. Doxdirect can add bleed for you, but it does that by stretching the edges, so it's better to supply it.
- **No crop marks.** Doxdirect wants the file at its exact size (1:1), and crop marks would make it bigger.
- **Resolution:** 300 dpi at full size. Their minimum is 150.
- **Colour:** Doxdirect prints in CMYK on a Canon digital press, and doesn't say what it wants you to send.
  - Best: export **CMYK, "Coated FOGRA39"**, and look at the result before uploading. Bright screen colours like tomato and cobalt shift a little in print, and this way you see the shift first.
  - If your design app can't export CMYK, send RGB and they'll convert it.
- **Fonts:** embedded, which PDF export does by default, or converted to outlines.
- **QR code:** at least 25 mm square on A4 (35 mm on A3), dark cocoa on cream, with a clear margin around it. Test it with a phone from the printed proof size.
- **What to choose on Doxdirect's page** ([posters](https://www.doxdirect.com/print-and-bind-online/posters/)):
  - Product: "A4 Poster" and then "A3 Poster" (add both to one basket)
  - Colour
  - Single sided
  - **300gsm Satin**
  - **No laminating**
  - Copies: 10 and 5 (or 20 and 10)
  - Standard 2-day production

If you switch to the runner-up, Instantprint asks for the same: 3 mm bleed, 3 mm safe zone, CMYK, 300 dpi, print-ready PDF (PDF/X-1a preferred).

## Each option in a few lines

**Doxdirect** ([posters](https://www.doxdirect.com/print-and-bind-online/posters/) · [delivery](https://www.doxdirect.com/help/delivery/) · [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/doxdirect.com))
- **Paper:** 160gsm plain, 170 / 300 / 400gsm satin or plain, optional matt or gloss lamination. The calculator's paper list differs from the "180 matt / 190 silk" text on the same page; the calculator is the live one.
- **Price and delivery:** no minimum order. Free tracked delivery from £10, otherwise £3.49.
- **Production:** 2 days as standard. Next-day and same-day production also exist; same-day needs the order in by 3pm on a weekday or 11am on Saturday. Their pages don't show what faster production costs.
- **Complaints:** occasional late or lost parcels and the odd print mix-up.
- **VAT:** charged at 20%.

**Instantprint** ([posters](https://www.instantprint.co.uk/posters) · [delivery](https://www.instantprint.co.uk/delivery-and-turnaround))
- **Paper:** 150gsm silk (the default), or 250 / 350gsm silk with optional lamination.
- **Price and delivery:** no minimum order. Delivery is £4.99, or free over £75 before VAT.
- **Files:** 3 mm bleed, 3 mm safe zone, CMYK, PDF/X-1a.
- **Complaints:** occasional courier delays and trimming slips.

**Solopress** ([A4](https://www.solopress.com/posters/a4/) · [A3](https://www.solopress.com/posters/a3/))
- **Paper:** silk, gloss or uncoated, 130–350gsm (the default is 130gsm gloss).
- **Price and delivery:** delivery is free, but there's a high fixed cost per line: 1 A4 is £15 and 100 A4 is £27. Great for 100+, poor for 10.
- **Files:** 3 mm bleed, CMYK FOGRA39.
- **Reviews:** 4.8 from 39,000.

**Vistaprint** ([posters](https://www.vistaprint.co.uk/signs-posters/posters/promotional-posters) · [shipping](https://www.vistaprint.co.uk/shipping))
- **Paper:** 130gsm matte (the default), 170gsm premium matte, 210gsm premium gloss, laminated or waterproof.
- **Price and delivery:** the Black Friday discount ends 1 Dec. Standard delivery is slow (about 6 working days).
- **Files:** only **1.5 mm bleed**, 300 dpi.

**Helloprint** ([posters](https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/posters) · [A3](https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/postersa3))
- **Paper:** silk or gloss at 135 / 170 / 250gsm, plus photo and PVC.
- **Price and delivery:** no minimum order. Delivery is free only over £60.
- **Files:** 3 mm bleed, 4 mm safe zone, CMYK FOGRA39.
- **Dates:** files must be approved by Mon 30 Nov 11:30 to arrive Fri 4 Dec.

**Printed.com** ([posters](https://www.printed.com/signage/posters))
- **Paper:** 250gsm silk or 120gsm uncoated only.
- **Price and delivery:** about twice Instantprint's price. A4 and A3 may go in separate delivery groups, each with its own charge.

**Route1Print** ([posters](https://www.route1print.co.uk/posters/posters))
- **Paper:** silk 115–350gsm, LED-UV inkjet. Delivery is included in the price.
- **Ordering:** trade-only (vetted print resellers), so probably not open to us.

**Flyerzone** ([posters](https://www.flyerzone.co.uk/instant-prices/silk-premium-saver-posters))
- **Paper:** 130gsm silk or gloss only, at this price.
- **Price and delivery:** above 10 copies it only sells in steps of 25. Free delivery is slow.
- **Files:** 1.5 mm bleed and a 10 mm quiet zone.

**Print Express** ([posters](https://printexpress.co.uk/discounted-posters/))
- London-based. No small-run A4.
- Charges £9.95 delivery under £25, and has no reviews.

**Mixam** ([posters](https://mixam.co.uk/posters))
- **Paper:** lots of paper choices.
- **Price and delivery:** no minimum order. Prices only show in its browser calculator. Different sizes usually ship with separate postage.

**Prodigi** (our supplier)
- Its plain "budget poster" (170gsm silk) and art papers (200gsm matte, 240gsm lustre) are made for one-off art sales, not promo runs.
- About £3–£5 per A4 before post. A3 ordered alone is made in Sweden.

**Ryman** ([print](https://www.ryman.co.uk/print-your-document) · [poster](https://www.ryman.co.uk/services/poster))
- **Paper:** 170gsm silk document print, or a 200gsm satin A3 poster service (about £10–£12 each).
- **Collection:** about 60 minutes, at 180+ shops.
- **VAT:** not confirmed. The site blocked our check, so figures come from its search listings.

**Max Spielmann / Asda Photo / Tesco Photo** ([poster prints](https://www.maxphoto.co.uk/photo-printing/poster-printing))
- One company, one price list: A4 £9, A3 £15, 15% off for Black Friday.
- **Paper:** 260gsm photo paper, printed to the edge.
- **Collection:** about 1 hour. You need an account to order.

**Snappy Snaps**
- Like Max Spielmann, but each branch sets its own prices (A3 is £15–£25).

**Timpson**
- Wall art is made centrally, so no same-day posters, and no prices published.

**Boots Photo**
- Good Fuji papers, but prices are hidden in the page code and collection takes 4–6 days.

**Local copy shops / university print rooms**
- Often the cheapest for one-offs: A3 about 60p–£2 on a laser printer, or £4.50–£5 on 200gsm poster paper.
- Quality and opening hours vary, and university rooms may not serve the public.
