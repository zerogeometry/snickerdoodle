# UK/EU print-on-demand and dropship makers for paper, prints, frames and games (alternatives to Prodigi)

Research date: 26 November 2026. Every figure below was read on 26 Nov 2026 unless a page date is given. Currency: GBP; the one EUR figure is converted at EUR 1 = GBP 0.87. Research only: no sign-ups, no forms, no uploads, no contact. "Needs account" means the price sits behind a trade login or a sales call. Gelato was skipped because another researcher covers it.

Overall picture: no single UK/EU maker found covers the whole range with a public price list and an order API. The strongest single leads are **theprintspace / creativehub** (London, API, framed prints, public prices), **All Jigsaw Puzzles trade** (UK, jigsaws, API/CSV), **Ivory Graphics** (UK playing cards, batch only, no API), **WTTB** and **Tradeprint Connect** (UK trade printers with white-label APIs; catalogue and prices need an account), and **Inkthreadable** (UK, API, cards, but only A6/A5 cards that are blank inside). CloudPrinter and Peecho are routing networks with free APIs, but their prices need an account and their UK production is unconfirmed.

## Framed prints (11×14, 12×16, 18×24, 24×32; collage frames): makers, prices, delivery, quality, API

### Takeaway
theprintspace / creativehub (London) is the best-documented UK option. It has a dropship API, a 4.8 Trustpilot score from about 12,000 reviews, wood frames, glass glazing (acrylic on the largest sizes), and public prices. Its direct framed prices are high: £71.70 ex VAT for an 11×14 standard thin frame, and its UK framed delivery is £13–£21 per frame. It does not offer 18×24 or 24×32 (nearest are 20×24 and 20×30), has only about 3–9 frame colours rather than 8, and uses glass rather than shatterproof glazing for most sizes. No collage or multi-photo frame maker was found.

**Inkthreadable (UK)**
- It lists fine art prints and framed fine art prints in its API catalogue.
- The API is free with no setup fee. Orders are sent in JSON or XML, with designs referenced by URL. Monthly-volume reward tiers give discounts.
- Production is in the UK, taking 3–5 working days. Shipping options are regular, recorded or courier.
- No frame prices or specs were retrieved.
- Source: [Inkthreadable API page, read 26 Nov 2026](https://www.inkthreadable.co.uk/with-api); docs: [Inkthreadable API help](https://help.inkthreadable.co.uk/en/collections/8562487-inkthreadable-s-api)

**Peecho (NL)**
- Its picture frames are sold through a separate site, readymades.co. Its wall art includes aluminium dibond and canvas.
- The API is free with no integration fees. Peecho says it prints in "30+ countries", but the UK is not named.
- Source: [Peecho print API, read 26 Nov 2026](https://www.peecho.com/print-api)

### Inferences
- theprintspace's direct framed prices (£72–£145 ex VAT, plus £13–£21 delivery) are premium art-lab prices. They are probably well above Prodigi's classic frame cost for the same sizes. Creativehub dropship pricing may differ from the direct price list; a creativehub account is needed to see it.
- Snickerdoodle's spec needs 8 frame colours and shatterproof glazing. Neither fits theprintspace's pre-made range (black, white, oak; glass except the largest sizes). Using it would mean a product-spec change.
- For a child's room, glass is a safety consideration. The acrylic-only sizes are the largest ones, so smaller sizes would ship with glass.

### Gaps
- No UK/EU maker was found for multi-photo or collage frames (12×12, 16×24, 24×36) with an API.
- Not researched for lack of tool budget: Loxley Colour, Photobox B2B, Desenio.
- Creativehub's actual dropship (not direct) frame prices need an account.
- Tracking on theprintspace UK dropship parcels is not stated.

## Greetings cards 7×5″ folded, ~330 gsm, printed inside, with envelope; packs of 5 and 10

### Takeaway
No UK/EU maker was confirmed to offer a 7×5″ folded card, printed outside and inside, through a public API. Inkthreadable (UK, API) is the closest. Its cards are A6 or A5 on 300 gsm matte, blank inside, £2.52 inc VAT, posted for £1.01 (Royal Mail 2nd class) or £1.51 (1st class). That is not the spec: they are not printed inside and not 7×5. The UK trade printers WTTB and Tradeprint Connect have white-label APIs and card ranges, but their exact card SKUs and prices need an account.

### Cited findings
- **Inkthreadable (UK)** card spec:
  - Sizes: A6 (105×148 mm) or A5 (148×210 mm), 300 gsm matte, blank inside, white envelope.
  - Packaging: a compostable film bag inside a rigid mailer.
  - Price: £2.52 inc VAT per card.
  - UK post: Royal Mail 2nd class £1.01 or 1st class £1.51, both untracked. More than one card can ship together on tracked methods, but tracked prices are not shown.
  - Printed in the UK.
  - Source: [Inkthreadable greeting cards, read 26 Nov 2026](https://www.inkthreadable.co.uk/greeting-cards)
- **WTTB (UK trade printer) print API:**
  - A sandbox, API documentation given on sign-up, and white-label dispatch direct to customers.
  - 140+ products, including "Cards & Stationery" and "Event & Occasion Print". Specific card SKUs are not listed.
  - Production is through a UK trade manufacturing network.
  - Needs an active WTTB account and your own developer. Fees and minimums are not stated.
  - Source: [WTTB print API, read 26 Nov 2026](https://wttb.co.uk/print-api)
- **Tradeprint Connect (UK):**
  - A white-label API that makes each order and ships it to the end customer. Tradeprint claims thousands of products.
  - Onboarding is through a sales call (Sales@tradeprint.co.uk).
  - The drop-ship page names canvas, clothing and mugs; it does not confirm greeting cards.
  - Third parties: a Node SDK on npm, and a Linkaprint Shopify app at $62/month.
  - Sources: [Tradeprint Connect help](https://help.tradeprint.co.uk/tradeprint-connect-the-uks-print-api); [Tradeprint drop shipping](https://replatform.tradeprint.co.uk/additional-services/drop-shipping); [Linkaprint Shopify app](https://apps.shopify.com/linkaprint-tradeprint-connect) (search summaries, 26 Nov 2026; the direct fetch returned 403)
- **Print.com (NL, possibly confused with "Printed.com")**: a reseller API with neutral packaging, made in two Dutch production hubs, shipping across the EU with 10 delivery services. Onboarding is by request. — [Print.com API, read 26 Nov 2026](https://www.print.com/en/api/)
- **Mixam (UK)**: no minimum quantity, and cards can be delivered flat or folded. No API was confirmed. — [Mixam greeting cards, search snippet 26 Nov 2026](https://mixam.co.uk/greetingcards)
- **Instantprint (UK)**: delivers greetings cards pre-folded. It is a standard print service, not dropship. — [Instantprint, search snippet 26 Nov 2026](https://www.instantprint.co.uk/estate-agents/new-home-cards)
- **Peecho**: greeting cards are not listed on its API page. An undated Printbox interview only said Peecho was "looking at" cards. — [Peecho print API, read 26 Nov 2026](https://www.peecho.com/print-api); [Printbox blog](https://www.getprintbox.com/blog/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/3063)

### Inferences
- For printed-inside 7×5 cards on Fedrigoni-class stock, a UK trade printer API (WTTB, Tradeprint) is the likely route. It needs an account and a sales conversation, and a multi-page or two-sided PDF would be needed.
- Packs of 5 or 10 different designs would probably be several line items in one order. Whether they ship in one envelope needs confirming.

### Gaps
- Not researched: Moo API status, Printed Easy, Photo Card Boutique, Inkifi B2B. No results surfaced for them within the tool budget.
- WTTB and Tradeprint card specs (gsm, 7×5 folded, inside print), prices and per-order delivery charges are all behind an account.

## Playing cards (54-card deck in a box) and jigsaws (30/110/252 pieces, tin or box)

### Takeaway
- **Jigsaws:** the best UK lead is the All Jigsaw Puzzles trade arm. It prints the puzzle and box on demand, takes orders by API or daily CSV (HP SiteFlow), has no minimums and quotes a 3–5 day turnaround. Prices need a trade account. Contrado (UK, API) offers puzzles in tins, but only 30 and 96 pieces.
- **Playing cards:** UK-made decks exist (Ivory Graphics, Agile Stationery), but no UK API dropship deck was found. Ivory is batch-priced: 50 poker decks with tuck boxes cost £341.10 ex VAT, about £6.82 each. MakePlayingCards and PrinterStudio are Hong Kong-based with China production: **flag, made outside the UK/EU.**

### Cited findings
- **All Jigsaw Puzzles trade (UK):**
  - Dropship with no minimums, print-on-demand custom puzzles, orders by API or daily CSV through HP SiteFlow.
  - Pricing needs a trade account.
  - Part of the SMART Games and Toys group since 2024.
  - A case study describes the puzzle and a matching branded box printed on demand and dispatched in 3–5 days.
  - Sources: [tradejigsaws about us](https://tradejigsaws.alljigsawpuzzles.co.uk/pages/about-us); [case study](https://tradejigsaws.alljigsawpuzzles.co.uk/blogs/case-studies/drop-shipping-an-artists-puzzle); [Oxfordshire photographer case study](https://tradejigsaws.alljigsawpuzzles.co.uk/blogs/case-studies/drop-shipping-for-a-small-oxfordshire-photographer) (search summaries, 26 Nov 2026; the dropship page returned 404 on direct fetch)
- **Contrado (UK):**
  - Jigsaws come in a printed or plain tin, or a satin bag, in 30 or 96 pieces. — [Contrado jigsaw puzzles UK, snippet 26 Nov 2026](https://www.contrado.com/jigsaw-puzzles-uk)
  - The API has docs at api.contrado.app/helix/docs. It is live on Shopify and WooCommerce, with Etsy "coming soon". There is no minimum order, production is in-house in the UK, and no webhooks are mentioned. — [Contrado print-on-demand API, read 26 Nov 2026](https://au.contrado.com/print-on-demand-api)
- **Printful UK**: offers puzzles with a tin or cardboard box. A user review complained about weak tin packaging. — [Printful UK puzzles, snippet 26 Nov 2026](https://www.printful.com/uk/custom-puzzles)
- **Ivory Graphics (UK):**
  - Has made custom playing cards in the UK since 1994, to UKCA, CE and GPSR standards. It says "from one pack upwards".
  - A company reply to a review gives a 50-pack minimum for boxes on 56-card packs.

- **Doxzoo (UK)**: its personalised playing cards do not come with boxes. It has a £5 minimum product total and free UK courier over £30. — [Doxzoo playing cards](https://doxzoo.com/products/playing-cards); [Doxzoo personalised playing cards](https://doxzoo.com/documents/personalised-playing-cards) (snippets, 26 Nov 2026)

## Data protection headline and cross-cutting API notes

### Takeaway
- Confirmed UK production: theprintspace/creativehub, Inkthreadable, Contrado, WTTB, Tradeprint and Ivory.
- EU: Print.com (NL hubs). Peecho and CloudPrinter route to partners in 30+ or 104 countries, so the actual printer country depends on routing.
- Outside the UK/EU: MakePlayingCards and PrinterStudio (Hong Kong/China).
- No DPA or GDPR pages were retrieved in this pass.

### Cited findings
- Production location by maker:
  - Contrado: "In House UK Production". — [Contrado API](https://au.contrado.com/print-on-demand-api)
  - Inkthreadable: printed, packed and shipped from the UK. — [Inkthreadable API](https://www.inkthreadable.co.uk/with-api)
  - WTTB: a UK trade manufacturing network. — [WTTB API](https://wttb.co.uk/print-api)
  - Print.com: two Dutch production hubs. — [Print.com API](https://www.print.com/en/api/)
  - Peecho: printing partners in 30+ countries, routed near the destination. — [Peecho](https://www.peecho.com/print-api)
  - CloudPrinter: 381+ providers in 104 countries. — [CloudPrinter](https://www.cloudprinter.com/print-api)
- Webhooks are explicitly offered by CloudPrinter (CloudSignal). They are not mentioned on the Peecho, Contrado, Inkthreadable or WTTB pages. — sources as above.

### Inferences
- For children's images, makers with confirmed in-house UK production (Contrado, Inkthreadable, theprintspace, WTTB, Tradeprint) are the simplest under the UK/EU-first residency rule.
- Peecho and CloudPrinter routing would need a contract term pinning UK orders to UK or EU printers.

### Gaps
- DPA and GDPR pages for each maker were not checked.
- Whether CloudPrinter or Peecho route UK orders to UK printers is unconfirmed.
