## 1. Price of the closest equivalent per product (blank, inc/ex VAT)

## 2. UK delivery: service, tracking, per order / per extra item, combined parcels

### Takeaway
Inkthreadable charges **by total parcel weight, not per item**. All UK orders are tracked: Royal Mail Tracked 48 is £3.78 for 101–699 g (one to about six tees), and DX courier is £11.99 per box up to 6 kg. Mixed products go in one parcel by weight, which is cheap for multi-item orders. Contrado charges £5.99 UK delivery. Print Clever (via Printify) charges $3.59 first plus $1.99 per extra tee, and $7.89 plus $3.59 per cushion. Teemill-made shops typically charge £5 standard.

### Cited Findings
- **Inkthreadable UK table** ("Page updated 4th Mar 2026"):

  - Royal Mail 24 Tracked: £3.48, £4.98, £7.38 for the same bands, 1–3 days.
  - DX courier: £11.99, 1 day. "Parcels over 2kg must be shipped with a courier service", one box up to 6 kg, then repeat charge per 6 kg.

## 3. Plans, fees, minimums, setup costs

### Takeaway
Inkthreadable has no plan fee and no API fee. Volume discounts run 5–10% from £500 a month spend. Contrado and Bags of Love dropship and wholesale discounts are 20–40% off retail by items per order, with some products capped at 20% or 30%. Teemill has a free Integration account and Pro at £10 a month (third-party figure). Printify Premium gives up to 20% off base prices for a monthly fee; $20 a month comes from a testimonial only. No minimums anywhere.

### Cited Findings
- **Inkthreadable**:
  - Free registration; "The API is free to use and there's no setup fee." — [Inkthreadable API](https://www.inkthreadable.co.uk/with-api)
  - Reward tiers by monthly eligible spend, which excludes shipping and fulfilment: Innovate £0 (monthly 10% sample code), Launch £500 = 5%, Develop £1,500 = 8%, Scale £2,500 = 9%, Momentum £7,500 = 10%. — [Inkthreadable reward pricing](https://www.inkthreadable.co.uk/reward-pricing)
  - Orders of more than 25 units of one design to one address are quoted separately. — [Inkthreadable FAQ](https://help.inkthreadable.co.uk/en/articles/435582-more-frequently-asked-questions)
- **Contrado** wholesale tiers by finished products per order: 1–10 up to 20%, 11–20 up to 25%, 21–49 up to 30%, 50+ up to 40%. "Some products are capped at a maximum of 20% or 30%." Items under $15 are excluded. — [Contrado wholesale guide](https://www.contrado.com/faq/148)
- Older Contrado dropship page: "at least 20% scaling to 40%" off standard pricing. — [Contrado drop-shipping FAQ](https://www.contrado.com/faq/226)
- The wholesale landing page advertises "20-50%", which conflicts with the FAQ's 40% top tier. — [Contrado wholesale](https://www.contrado.co.uk/wholesale)
- Bags of Love: "between 20% and 40% discount on most of their orders", with the same tiers. — [Bags of Love stores guide](https://bagsoflove.com/faq/275); [Bags of Love wholesale guide](https://www.bagsoflove.co.uk/faq/201)

- At a small shop's early volumes (under £500 a month at Inkthreadable, or 1–10 items per Contrado order), expect Inkthreadable's list price and Contrado retail minus 20%.

- Whether Contrado discounts apply to API orders isn't stated on the API page.
- The current Printify Premium fee in GBP wasn't confirmed.

## 4. API and automation (order API, webhooks, files, docs) — integration effort for a custom Next.js shop

### Takeaway
Inkthreadable and Contrado both have direct order APIs suitable for a custom Next.js shop. Inkthreadable is simple: one POST with product part number, design **URL**, address and shipping method, authenticated by AppId plus Signature. No webhooks were documented, so polling would be needed. Contrado's API (docs at api.contrado.app/helix/docs) uploads artwork with the order and offers "real-time" tracking, but webhooks aren't confirmed. Teemill has a Print API with an API key. Print Clever and T Shirt and Sons are only reachable through Printify's API or third-party tools (OrderDesk), not directly.

### Cited Findings
- **Inkthreadable** API:
  - Orders go to `POST /api/orders.php` (JSON or XML).
  - Other endpoints: `GET /api/orders.php` (filters status, since_id, dates), `GET /api/order.php?id=`, `GET /api/orders/count.php`, and `DELETE` for unpaid orders only.
  - Auth is `AppId=…&Signature=…` query parameters.
  - Each item has `designs.front` and `designs.back` image **URLs**, plus a `label` object (type "printed").
  - No webhooks are mentioned.
  - — [Inkthreadable API endpoints](https://help.inkthreadable.co.uk/en/articles/429293-api-request-endpoints)
  - "Orders reference products by part number and designs by URL, so there are no file uploads to manage". Docs collection: https://help.inkthreadable.co.uk/en/collections/8562487-inkthreadable-s-api — [Inkthreadable API page](https://www.inkthreadable.co.uk/with-api)
  - Integrations also exist for Shopify, WooCommerce, Wix, TikTok Shop, BigCartel and Squarespace. — [Inkthreadable help: shipping keywords](https://help.inkthreadable.co.uk/en/articles/7183000-shipping-keywords-for-ecommerce-integrations)
- **Contrado** API:
  - "your system automatically sends order data, artwork files and shipping details through the API". Orders go straight to production, with "track orders in real time".
  - Docs: **https://api.contrado.app/helix/docs**. Developer sign-up is at /estore/developer/signup.
  - No webhooks are mentioned.
  - — [Contrado print-on-demand API](https://www.contrado.co.uk/print-on-demand-api)
- **Teemill**:
  - An API page ("Teemill Print API") and a docs page ("Print-on-Demand API Documentation") exist but are JS-rendered and couldn't be read. — [Teemill API](https://teemill.com/print-on-demand-api/); [Teemill API docs](https://teemill.com/api-docs/)
  - Third-party clients show an API key in the Authorization header, v1 platform order routes (create, confirm, get, PATCH), and a separate `https://teemill.com/omnis/v3` custom-product endpoint that "returns a product page". These sources conflict on which surface applies. — [@teemill/platform npm](https://www.pkgstats.com/pkg:@teemill/platform); [Polotno Teemill demo](https://github.com/polotno-project/polotno-teemill-demo); [Polotno Teemill integration](https://polotno.com/sdk/product/integrations/teemill)
- **Printify** has a public developer API covering all providers, Print Clever and T Shirt and Sons included. — [Printify developers](https://developers.printify.com/)
  - Print Clever's own Shopify app "may bill external charges separately". — [Print Clever Shopify app](https://apps.shopify.com/print-clever)
- **T Shirt and Sons** can be reached via OrderDesk, a third-party order manager. — [OrderDesk Tshirt & Sons integration](https://help.orderdesk.com/integration-setup-guides/print-on-demand/tshirt-and-sons-integration)
- **Gelato** (a network of UK partners, not a UK maker itself) has a REST API with a sandbox and webhook events on orders. UK partner names and textile coverage weren't stated. — [Gelato integrate](https://www.gelato.com/uk/gelato-platinum/integrate); [Gelato features](https://www.gelato.com/au/features)
- **Fifth Column** (London) runs a Kornit Atlas MAX PLUS on a click model; no public API was found. — [Kornit Fifth Column case](https://www.kornit.com/?p=17792)

### Inferences
- **Integration effort, Inkthreadable: low.** It is a single REST-style POST, and design-by-URL fits the existing server-side signed-URL pattern. Status updates would need a scheduled poll (for example, a `jobs` task querying `since_id` and status), since no webhooks are documented. The signed URL must live long enough for Inkthreadable to fetch it.
- **Integration effort, Contrado: moderate.** There is a dedicated API with file upload, but the docs weren't read in detail.
- **Integration effort, Printify (for Print Clever or T Shirt and Sons): moderate.** It is one more intermediary, and Printify's product IDs map per provider.

### Gaps
- Whether Inkthreadable or Contrado offer webhooks or tracking callbacks; the docs weren't fully read.
- Contrado's file requirements (DPI, format) and auth method.
- Teemill's real order-API capabilities, and whether it can fulfil orders for an external checkout rather than only Teemill-hosted stores.
- Direct APIs for "Prima Printing", "Duplium" (UK), "Stitch & Print", "Print A Sock", "Solesmith" and "Sock Factory": no search time was left to check these.

## 5. Quality evidence (reviews, print method, durability, organic certification)

### Takeaway
Inkthreadable scores 4.3 on Trustpilot from 953 reviews, with some recent print-defect complaints. Contrado scores about 4.1 from about 1.9–2k reviews, with mixed comments on defects and delays. Print Clever scores 2.8 from 25 reviews, with complaints focused on Printify-routed delays. Teemill reviews praise print quality, but some say the fabric is thin and Royal Mail tracking is poor. Methods: DTG for tees, dye-sublimation for socks, tea towels and blankets, and DTF for Inkthreadable's cushion. Organic claims exist, but certifiers were only named for Teemill (GOTS) tea towels.

### Cited Findings
- **Inkthreadable** Trustpilot:
  - TrustScore **4.3 from 953 reviews**: 73% 5-star, 8% 1-star, about 100 reviews in the last 12 months.
  - Recent complaints include "extremely wonky, with poor quality print", a wrong design, and 11 missing shirts.
  - It replies to 26% of negative reviews.
  - — [Trustpilot Inkthreadable](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/www.inkthreadable.co.uk)
  - Inkthreadable's own banner claims "900+ Trustpilot reviews rated 4.6" and "2M+ orders fulfilled". — [Inkthreadable](https://www.inkthreadable.co.uk/update-shipping-costs-page)
- Inkthreadable warns that exact positioning "can't be guaranteed with dye sublimation" for socks. — via search summary of [Inkthreadable sublimation socks](https://inkthreadable.co.uk/new-products-sublimation-socks)
- **Contrado** Trustpilot: about **4.1**, 1,923 to "2K" reviews depending on snapshot. It replied to 70–88% of negatives. — [Trustpilot Contrado](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/contrado.co.uk?page=2)
- **Print Clever** Trustpilot: **2.8 from 25 reviews** (UK page), or 2.4 from 28 (NL mirror). Complaints centre on Printify-routed orders delayed for weeks. Location is possibly Swindon, according to a review, unconfirmed. — [Trustpilot Print Clever](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/printclever.com)
- **Teemill**:
  - Trustpilot reviews mention thin fabric ("not good value at £22+"), Royal Mail tracking problems with free reships, and good print quality. — [Teemill Trustpilot p.4](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/teemill.com?page=4)
  - Teemill-made tea towels are described as GOTS-certified organic cotton. — [Bats tea towel](https://bats-without-borders.teemill.com/product/love-bats-tea-towel/)
- Printify lists T Shirt and Sons (Westbury, fulfilment centre), Print Clever and Shirt Monkey as its UK providers. — [Printify UK](https://printify.com/custom-t-shirts/uk/)
- A Shopify community user rated Printful socks "not very good" (single opinion). — [Shopify community: POD socks](https://community.shopify.com/t/looking-for-good-quality-print-on-demand-socks/352762)

### Inferences
- Inkthreadable's own claim (4.6) is higher than the live Trustpilot figure (4.3). The gap may reflect fewer review invitations recently, since Trustpilot notes it hasn't invited customers lately.
- Ordering samples is the real quality test. Inkthreadable's monthly 10% sample code would lower that cost.

### Gaps
- No wash-durability evidence was found for any maker.
- No Reddit threads were checked (time).
- The organic certifier for Stanley/Stella blanks wasn't confirmed on maker pages, though Stanley/Stella is generally GOTS-certified; not verified here.
- Teemill's overall Trustpilot score and count weren't captured.

## 6. Own-label options (neck labels, packaging) and costs

### Takeaway
Inkthreadable supports printed neck labels through the API's `label` object, plus custom packing slips and inserts (inserts from the Scale tier, £2,500 a month). Prices aren't public. Contrado is white-label by default, with logo, brand name and thank-you message tools. Printify printed neck labels cost about $2 per item.

### Cited Findings

## 7. Headline data protection and origin (UK-made? DPA/GDPR page?)

### Takeaway
Inkthreadable, Contrado, Teemill, Print Clever and T Shirt and Sons all print in the UK. **Duplium is Canadian, not UK**, so it's flagged as out of scope for a UK-first shop. Gelato and Printify are routers, and the factory depends on the provider chosen. I didn't check DPA or GDPR pages for any maker (time).

### Cited Findings
- Inkthreadable "prints, packs and ships from the UK". — [Inkthreadable API](https://www.inkthreadable.co.uk/with-api)
- Contrado: products are manufactured at Contrado's UK facilities. — [Contrado API](https://www.contrado.co.uk/print-on-demand-api)
- Teemill: orders "are automatically sent to our UK factories". It also has EU operations for EU and Northern Ireland shipping. — [Teemill API search snippet](https://teemill.com/print-on-demand-api/); [St Andrews shop](https://shop.st-andrews.ac.uk/pages/delivery-information)
- **Duplium is located in Canada** with high shipping costs. Printify's help groups it as "Duplium (CA)". — via search summary of [Printify priority shipping help](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/20166678471185-How-do-I-upgrade-my-order-to-Priority-Shipping)
- T Shirt and Sons fulfilment centre is in Westbury, UK. — [Printify UK](https://printify.com/custom-t-shirts/uk/)
- Printify says you must "manually choose a UK-based Print Provider". — via search summary of [Printify UK blog](https://printify.com/blog/why-printify-thrives-in-the-uk-pod-market/)

### Inferences
- Design-by-URL (Inkthreadable) means a child's drawing leaves the shop's private bucket via a signed URL, the same as with Prodigi. The DPA and retention terms of any new maker would need the same Article 28 review before switching.
- Printify adds a US-headquartered intermediary that holds order data and artwork, even when the factory is in the UK. That is a heavier transfer review than going direct to Inkthreadable or Contrado.

- DPA, GDPR or privacy pages and artwork-retention terms for Inkthreadable, Contrado, Teemill and Printify weren't checked.
