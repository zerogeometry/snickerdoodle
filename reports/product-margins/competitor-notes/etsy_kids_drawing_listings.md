# Etsy UK prices for products made from a child's own drawing (seen 26 Nov 2026)

**How these were collected (read first)**
- Etsy blocks plain fetching (HTTP 403 to WebFetch). So every price below comes from the **live Etsy UK site** (`etsy.com/uk/search?...&ship_to=GB`), loaded in a real browser on 26 Nov 2026, plus each listing page fetched from inside that browser session. **No prices come from search-engine snippets.** The one exception is a single Bing/Google-style summary I looked at early on; I did not use it for any row.
- **Price** = the price on the search card (GBP), after any sale. Etsy shows the **cheapest option** ("from" price). Where I read the listing's option menu, I give the option prices too. Several "from" prices are for a cheaper item in the same listing (for example a coaster inside a "mug" listing). I flag those.
- **UK delivery** = the card's "£X incl. postage" total minus the item price, or "free" where the card says "Free delivery". This is delivery for one item to a GB address.
- **Shop sales** = the "X sales" figure on the listing page. That is the **whole shop's lifetime sales, not this listing's.** Etsy does not show per-listing sales.
- **Reviews** = `reviewCount` from the listing page's structured data. This is **unreliable**: for some listings it is clearly the shop's total (for example, two different GiftedBoxKeepsakes listings both show 4,003). Treat it as a rough signal only.
- "BEST" = the listing showed Etsy's **Bestseller** badge. "Ad" = a paid search placement. "GB" = card said "Sent from GB". **non-GB** = no "Sent from GB" (overseas or print-on-demand), noted as such.
- This is the week of Black Friday / Cyber Monday, and Etsy's "Cyber Specials" were live. Many listings are 10–70% off, so normal prices are often higher (the original price is given where shown).
- Date seen for every row: **26 Nov 2026**. Prefix for all URLs: `https://www.etsy.com`.

## Key question 1: price and UK postage for each listing

### Takeaway
UK sellers usually charge a low item price with £2–£4.50 postage on top, or a slightly higher price with free delivery. Mugs, ornaments, coasters and single cards start around £3–£8. Tea towels and cushions sit around £8–£14. Tees, phone cases, prints and socks sit around £12–£26. Blankets, framed prints and premium handmade pieces run £17–£50+. Some types have almost no UK supply: calendars, height charts, Apple Watch straps, placemats and playing cards.

### Inferences
- **UK postage norm:** UK sellers add **£1.65–£4.50** (typically **£3.50–£4.00** for a mug or cushion, **£1.00–£3.05** for cards, **£1.65–£2.80** for ornaments). The other common pattern is "free delivery" built into a higher price (Jelliegiftco, FredAndBo, MaeRoseandRoo, MintPrintsGB, RusticFlitch). Non-GB sellers typically add **£6–£48** delivery, which makes them uncompetitive for UK buyers once postage is counted.
- **"From" prices mislead.** In several "mug" listings the headline price is the coaster or magnet. Engraved, embroidered and print-on-demand listings show heavy "was £39.97" discounts. Real like-for-like comparison needs the option menu.

### Gaps
- Per-listing sales counts are not shown by Etsy (only shop totals). Per-listing review counts from structured data are partly shop-level, so they cannot be read reliably as listing popularity.
- No UK seller was found printing a **child's own drawing** into a **calendar**, a **height chart** or an **Apple Watch strap**. Only one UK seller each was found for **playing cards** (MartinDesigns12), **placemats** (DinkyArtist) and drawing-specific **socks** (Solesmith). The "3+ listings" target could not be met for these, at least not with UK sellers.
- I did not use outside "Etsy pricing insight" articles. Every range below is from the listings above.
- Prices are Cyber-week sale prices for many listings. Normal (non-sale) prices are typically 10–30% higher for UK sellers.
- Etsy's search results are personalised and ad-ranked, so this is a sample of 10–40 listings per search, not a census.

### Inferences
- Drawing-based UK products cluster in three bands. **Under £10:** mugs from budget sellers, ornaments, coasters, single cards, wrapping sheets. **£10–£20:** most mugs, cushions, tea towels, kids' tees, prints, card packs, socks, playing cards, placemats. **£22–£50+:** framed A3, phone cases, adult embroidered tees, blankets, glass domes, handmade ceramics.
- On categories with thin UK supply (calendars, jigsaws, blankets, watch straps, socks), the non-UK sellers on Etsy UK charge high item prices plus £6–£48 delivery. A UK maker with domestic postage would be competitive there on landed price.

### Gaps
- The medians are rough: a hand count of the GB rows, not a statistical sample. For thin categories (jigsaw, blanket, socks, playing cards, placemat) the "typical" figure is a single seller.
- Size and variant standardisation differs (11oz vs 15oz mugs, cushion with or without inner, A5 vs A4 prints), so ranges mix sizes. Sizes are given where the listing stated them.

## Key question 3: are best-selling listings priced at the low or high end?

### Takeaway
The highest-volume UK shops sit at the **low end**, with a low headline price plus separate postage (£3–£6 item + about £4 post). Several Bestseller-badged or heavily reviewed listings sit in the **lower-middle** (£8–£15, often free delivery). A few premium handmade pieces also hold Bestseller badges at £36.50. Price leadership wins volume, but a mid-price with free delivery is clearly viable too.

### Inferences
- Etsy UK buyers of drawing gifts are price-sensitive. The volume leaders show a low sticker price (often under £5) and recover margin through postage. Shops pricing at £14–£17 with free delivery also sustain tens of thousands of sales, so a landed price of about £12–£18 for a mug-class item appears to be the mass-market sweet spot.
- Premium positioning works when the craft is visibly different (hand-painted wood, embroidery, glass dome, handmade stoneware). It does not work for a plain print-on-demand item.

### Gaps
- Shop-level sales mix all products, so "high sales" cannot be attributed to the specific listing or price point. Bestseller badges are Etsy's own opaque signal (recent sales relative to category).
- Overseas print-on-demand shops with huge review counts (MegaGraphicTees 12,508; MindBlowingStudio 19,262) may be showing shop-level review totals. Their volume on UK orders specifically is unknown.
