Conversion used throughout: €1 = £0.87, $1 = £0.79. GBP figures are approximate and rounded to the penny.
"Live" = page fetched directly today. "Snippet" = the live page was blocked (403/404) or not fetched, so the price comes from a search-engine result for that URL. A snippet may be older than today, so treat it as less reliable.
Pages that refused automated fetches today: budsies.com (403), minted.com category pages (403), artkivebox.com/pricing (404), and several guessed CEWE, Pixum, Smartphoto and Photobox URLs (404, wrong paths). For those sellers the figures are snippets.

## Key question 1: what is the typical price per product type in the US and the EU?

### Takeaway
Mass photo-gift sites charge about the same in the EU, the US and the UK. Typical figures: a mug about £9.50–12, a cushion £22–37 (small/medium), an A4 calendar £15–19, an ornament £8–10, a 500–1,000 piece jigsaw £37–46, and a basic printed tee £8–12. The real outliers are US framed prints, at about £67–98 for an 8x10 framed print at Shutterfly, and the US kids'-art specialists.

### Inferences
- Mugs: the EU mass price is about £9.50–11.50 for a standard mug and £17–19 for magic or porcelain mugs. US Zazzle drawing mugs sell for about £10.70–13.60 on sale. Shutterfly lists at about £15.80 but promotes heavily, often to $5.
- Calendars land at about £15–19 for A4 on both sides of the Atlantic. Ornaments start at about £8–10 at mass sites in both regions.
- Framed prints are where the US stands apart. Shutterfly's 8x10 framed print starts at about £67 on sale. EU mass sites lead with posters and canvases at about £5–24, and their framed-print prices were not captured.
- In the EU, sale and "best 30-day price" display is common. Photobox FR shows €6.99 against a "best 30-day" €13.99 because of EU price-indication rules. Headline "from" prices can therefore halve during promotions.

## Key question 2: how does the kids'-art specialist premium (Artkive, Minted, Budsies, Picture This) compare with the mass photo-gift sites?

### Takeaway
The specialist premium is large, about 3–7×, where the specialist adds craft or curation: illustration, sewing, book layout or framing. It is roughly zero on commodity print. A "your child's drawing" mug on Zazzle or Etsy costs the same as any photo mug.

### Cited Findings
- **Tees.** Picture This Clothing kids' tees start at $59 (£46.61) and adult tees at $69 (£54.51) — [picturethisclothing.com](https://www.picturethisclothing.com/). Etsy kids-drawing tees cost about $5–40, with one at $9.47 including free shipping — [etsy.com](https://www.etsy.com/market/child_drawing_shirt). Vistaprint DE's basic tee is €9 (£7.83) — [vistaprint.de](https://www.vistaprint.de/kleidung-taschen/t-shirts). The specialist is about 3–7× the mass or marketplace price.
- **Books.** An Artkive book starts at $75 (£59.25) for 25 pages, plus a $39 box if the art is sent in — [artkiveapp.com/pricing](https://artkiveapp.com/pricing). An Albelli M hardcover photo book is €27.99 (£24.35) — [albelli.nl](https://www.albelli.nl/prijsoverzicht). That is about 2.5×, before Artkive's box fee.
- **Framed art.** Minted's child's-drawing art print in a small frame (4x6 image) is about $104 (£82) — [minted.com](https://www.minted.com/product/custom-commissioned-art/MIN-2CJ-KCF/hand-painted-chevron) (search summary). Shutterfly's larger 8x10 framed print is $84.99–123.98 — [shutterfly.com](https://www.shutterfly.com/t/framed-prints/?dimensions=8x10). Per square inch of image, Minted costs roughly 2–3× Shutterfly. Against EU mass wall decor (Albelli canvas in a floating frame 30x20, €26.99 / £23.48 — [albelli.nl](https://www.albelli.nl/prijsoverzicht)) it is about 3.5×.
- **Framed mosaic.** Artkive's 14x14 is $99 and its 26x26 is $189–249 — [Artkive FAQ](https://faq.artkiveapp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038658331-All-About-Mosaics).
- **Plush.** Budsies is quoted at "from $99" (£78) — [demilked](https://www.demilked.com/?p=154285). Etsy drawing-to-plush runs €35.78–117.30 — [etsy](https://www.etsy.com/dk-en/r/similar/1732930720). The nearest mass product is CEWE's photo-shirt teddy at €13.95 — [fotoservice.ah.nl](https://fotoservice.ah.nl/fotocadeaus/knuffels.html). That teddy is a different, much simpler product, so it shows the floor rather than a like-for-like comparison.
- **Mugs.** Zazzle's "turn your child's artwork into a mug" sells at $13.56–17.17 on sale — [zazzle.com](https://www.zazzle.com/turn_your_childs_artwork_or_drawing_into_a_coffee_mug-168743509795201800). That is the same band as Zazzle's general photo mugs (about $15–16 on sale) — [zazzle.com](https://www.zazzle.com/baby+photo+mugs). So there is no drawing premium on marketplace mugs.
- **Ornaments.** Etsy kids-art ornaments mostly cost $8–20, with a premium slate version at $28+ — [etsy.com](https://www.etsy.com/market/kids_drawing_ornament). Shutterfly ornaments start at $9.99 — [shutterfly.com](https://www.shutterfly.com/deals/special-offers/). That is a modest premium, and only for premium materials.
- **Cards.** Minted works out at about $1.97–2.28 per card in volume — [Penny Hoarder](https://www.thepennyhoarder.com/save-money/holiday-greeting-cards/). Albelli is about €1.10 per single card in a pack of 10 — [albelli.nl](https://www.albelli.nl/prijsoverzicht). That is roughly 1.6–1.9× at the design-led brand.

### Inferences
- The premium follows how much transformation and craft is visible: an illustrated or foil print, a sewn plush, a curated book, a framed piece. It does not follow print cost. This matches the existing UK brief ("People pay 2–6× commodity prices when the child's art is 'made real'") in `project-office/briefs/PRICING-elasticity-premium.md`.
- On marketplaces (Zazzle, Etsy), "from your child's drawing" is a search keyword, not a price premium. Commodity items such as mugs and ornaments stay at mass price there.

### Gaps
- I could not see Artkive's mug or other merchandise prices (none found).
- Minted's own drawing-to-art pricing at sizes other than the small frame was not found.

## Key question 3: are UK prices higher or lower than EU/US, by roughly how much, per product type?

### Takeaway
For mass photo gifts the UK is at roughly parity with the EU, within about ±10% at the same brand, and with the US. The clear gaps are framed prints, where US prices run about 3–5× the UK mass "mounted print" anchor, and kids'-art specialist tees, where the US costs about 1.5–2× the UK drawing-niche band.

### Cited Findings

- **Mug, UK.** CEWE UK Classic Photo Mug starts at £10.99 — [cewe.co.uk](https://www.cewe.co.uk/photo-gifts/photo-mugs/standard-mug.html) (snippet). Photobox UK standard mug is £11.99 and wraparound £12.99 — [photobox.co.uk/price-overview](https://www.photobox.co.uk/price-overview) (snippet). The brief's UK mug anchor is £9.99–13, with Vistaprint UK at £14.99.

  - **Other markets.** EU NL mass is £9.56–10.43 (Albelli, Smartphoto), so the UK is about 10–20% higher there. US Zazzle drawing mugs are £10.71–13.56 on sale, about parity.
- **Cushion.** The brief gives Photobox UK at £32.99–56.99. Photobox FR starts at €37.99 (£33.05), which is parity. Albelli runs £23.48–55.67, and CEWE DE starts at £21.74. So at entry level, UK mass is about the same as or up to about 30% above the cheapest EU entry cushion.
- **Jigsaw.** The brief gives Photobox UK at £34.99–49.99. Photobox FR starts at €29.99 (£26.09) and Albelli runs £33.05–46.10. The UK is at parity to about 15–25% higher at entry.
- **Tee (mass).** The brief gives Vistaprint UK at £9. Vistaprint DE is €9 (£7.83), so the UK is about 15% higher in GBP terms. That fits a same-number pricing pattern (£9 vs €9).
- **Tee (drawing niche).** The brief's UK drawing-niche band is £26–35. US Picture This Clothing is £46.61–54.51, about 1.5–2× the UK. US Etsy is £7.50–20, lower than the UK niche.
- **Framed print.** The brief's UK anchor is Photobox mounted prints at £11.99–26.99. US Shutterfly 8x10 framed is £67–98 and Minted's drawing-print frame is about £82. These are not like-for-like (mounted vs framed), but US framed prices are about 3–5× the UK mass anchor. EU mass canvases and floating frames are £15.65–23.48 (Albelli), about parity with the UK mass anchor.
- **Greeting cards.** The brief's UK anchor is a single card at £3.99 (Moonpig, Funky Pigeon). The EU and US prices found are for packs of photo cards: Albelli at about £0.96–1.74 per card, Minted at about £1.56–1.80 per card. The formats differ (a single posted card vs a bulk pack), so no fair percentage can be given.

### Inferences
- In mass photo gifts, the UK, EU and US sit in the same band once currency is converted. A UK mug, cushion, jigsaw or calendar is unlikely to look expensive to an EU or US shopper, or cheap.
- The biggest overseas price headroom is in specialist "made from the drawing" products and framed wall art. In both, the US prices well above UK mass anchors.
