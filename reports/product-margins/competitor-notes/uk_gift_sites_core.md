# UK personalised / photo-gift sites: core upload-your-own product prices (seen 26 Nov 2026)

Method: live product and category pages read in a real browser on 26 Nov 2026, the week of Black Friday. All prices are GBP including VAT as shown on the page (Photobox, Vistaprint and Snapfish say "incl. VAT"). "Everyday" means the struck-through, regular or "was" price on the page. Whether these sites ever actually charge that price is a separate question (see Key question 1). Black Friday was running everywhere, so sale prices this week are deeper than usual. Delivery is UK standard for one item unless stated.

Sites I could not price, or only partly:
- **Truprint:** the browser refused truprint.co.uk and search found no Truprint pages.
- **The Personalised Gifts Shop:** thepersonalisedgiftsshop.co.uk would not load, and search found no matching retailer.
- **Boots Photo:** the photo-gifts page has no products on it (only breadcrumbs and footer).
- **Getting Personal:** gettingpersonal.co.uk now redirects to Card Factory.
- **Prezzybox:** prezzybox.com now redirects to Menkind.

## Key question 1: Everyday price vs sale price per product (dense price table)

### Takeaway
Every big photo-gift site showed a struck-through "regular" price next to a sale price, at 15% to 70% off. Some discounts are code-driven (Photobox EARLYBF26, 40% on mugs, cushions, jigsaws and cards and up to 70% on wall art; Vistaprint VISTA50; Printerpix XMAS2026). Others are always on (Snapfish shows about 50% off on almost everything; Printerpix shows 65–70% off against inflated "was" prices). The fixed-price card and gift shops (Funky Pigeon, Card Factory, Moonpig) price much lower and flatter: about £8–10 for a mug and £15–22 for a cushion.

- **Snapfish** shows "Regular Price" at about 2× the sale price on almost every product, and Printerpix shows 65–70% off. ## Key question 2: Delivery cost per order for each site

Standard UK delivery for one personalised gift runs about £2.69 (Snapfish) to £5.99 (Moonpig gifts, Bags of Love). Photobox is £3.99 and Vistaprint £4.99, with Vistaprint free over £40. Cards ship cheaply: Moonpig from £1.35. Vistaprint was the only site where I captured a free-delivery threshold for gifts.

### Inferences
- **Price-anchoring benchmark:** a mass-market UK parent can buy a photo mug for about £6–10 and a medium cushion for about £15–25 before delivery. Anything well above that needs a visible premium story, such as NOTHS-style handmade or "made from your child's drawing" framing (NOTHS child's-drawing keepsakes sell at £12–25).
- **Ceilings:** £15–24 for a mug, £35–40 for a cushion and £50–60 for a blanket on NOTHS mark the realistic "artisan / boutique" ceiling.
- **Framed prints** are the least commoditised line: big sites still show £40–60 now for 30×40 to 50×70cm. That category leaves the most headroom.

### Gaps
- Too few data points for kids' tees, adult tees and A5 calendars to call a reliable range.
- Large-blanket and large-frame bands rest on 3–4 sites each.
- Prices were captured on Black Friday week. Some sales end 26 Nov (Vistaprint) or 30 Nov (ASDA), so a re-check in early December would show the post-Black-Friday level.
