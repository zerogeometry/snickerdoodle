# UK specialist "child's drawing to product" sellers: prices seen 26 Nov 2026

Scope: UK specialist makers who turn a child's own drawing into a product (not general photo-gift sites, not Etsy). All prices GBP, consumer prices inc VAT unless marked "ex VAT". Date seen for every row: 26 Nov 2026. This is Black Friday week: many NOTHS listings and Dinky Artist's own site show a temporary sale price, so both the sale and the regular price are given where shown.

How this was gathered: sellers' own sites and their notonthehighstreet.com (NOTHS) listings, read live. Several sites blocked or failed automated reads (listed under Gaps). Product review counts on NOTHS product pages did not load ("Loading reviews"), so volume signals are mostly shop-level figures.

## Q1. What exact price does each specialist charge for each product type?

### Takeaway
The sellers that could be read charge about £12 to £15 for small items (coasters, keyrings, wooden baubles), £18 to £25 for mugs, kids' tees, tea towels and card sets, £30 to £36 for adult tees and cushions, and £50 to £55 for statement pieces (velvet cushions, slate boards, leather). School fundraiser firms sell per unit far lower: tea towels at about £2.35 to £5 each to the school (ex VAT), resold to parents at £6 to £7.50, and card packs at £7 to £9 per pack. Many product types in the brief (jigsaws, blankets, calendars, phone cases, socks, playing cards, wrapping paper, placemats, watch straps) were not found at these specialists.

## Q2. What do they charge for UK delivery, and is there a free-delivery threshold?

### Takeaway
On their own sites, lukedrewthis charges £3.50 to £5.50 for 1st class signed delivery with no free threshold stated, and Dinky Artist gives free delivery over £75. On NOTHS most of these sellers offer free standard delivery, or £2.79 to £3.99, with £4.99 for faster delivery. Hurleyburley gives free UK delivery with no minimum.

### Cited Findings
- lukedrewthis: 1st class signed £3.50 (large letter) or £5.50 (small parcel); next working day £7.95 / £9 / £11 by size; Saturday £12.95; non-mainland and Highlands £7.95; dispatch about 3 working days after artwork arrives (up to 5 in busy periods); no free threshold mentioned. — [lukedrewthis delivery](https://www.lukedrewthis.co.uk/pages/delivery)
