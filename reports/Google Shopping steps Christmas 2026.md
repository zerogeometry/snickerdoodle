# Google Shopping: the steps, Christmas 2026

Written 18 November 2026 for Simon. This goes with moves 1 and 4 in
[Christmas digital ads 2026](Christmas%20digital%20ads%202026.md). **Nothing has been set up or spent.** Every click below is
yours ("Your turn") unless it says "I'll do".

Google moves its menus around. If a label doesn't match, use the search box at the top of Merchant Center or Google Ads and
type the words in **bold**.

## Part 1: tidy the free listings (Merchant Center, about 1–2 hours, by Fri 20 Nov)

Open [Merchant Center](https://merchants.google.com/) and sign in with the account that holds the Snickerdoodle feed.

**Step 1: fix the warnings.**
1. In the left menu, click **Products**.
2. Open the **Needs attention** tab.
3. For each product listed, note the reason Google gives. Typical ones are "image too small", "price mismatch" or "missing
   shipping".
4. **Send me the list** (a screenshot is fine). I'll fix anything that comes from the feed or the product pages, and tell you
   which ones are clicks for you.

**Step 2: returns policy.**
1. Click the **gear icon** (settings), then **Shipping and returns** (it may say **Delivery and returns**).
2. Open the **Returns** tab. Google checks that this matches what the site says
   ([S](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/14011730)). Today the site's only wording is in
   [terms, section 6](https://snickerdoodle.co.uk/trust/terms): personalised items are made specially, and "statutory rights,
   including for faulty goods, are unaffected". There's no separate returns page.
3. Set the Merchant Center policy to match: **no returns on personalised items; faulty or damaged items put right**. Don't
   promise a returns window the site doesn't state.
4. **Your decision:** a short, plain returns page (what to do if something arrives damaged or wrong) would help Google's
   store-quality score and reassure first-time buyers. Say yes and I'll draft it for you to approve.

**Step 3: delivery times and the Christmas cut-offs.**
1. In the same settings area, open the **Shipping** (or **Delivery services**) tab.
2. Check the service is **United Kingdom**, **£0**, with handling and transit days that match the site.
3. Look for a **holiday cut-off** option on the service. If it's there, set Christmas to **Mon 14 Dec**, the frames, blankets
   and cushions date. Most other products close on 10–12 Dec.
4. **Watch out:** our feed sends its own delivery details on every product, and Google says product-level delivery details
   override the account's settings ([S](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6069284?hl=en-GB)). So a cut-off set here
   may not show on the listings. **Tell me what you see.** If it isn't showing, we'll simply stop the paid test on 14 Dec and
   lean on the site's order-by banners, and I'll look at whether the feed should carry the dates itself.

**Step 4: see what's getting seen.**
1. Click **Performance** (or **Analytics**) in the left menu.
2. Filter by **free listings**, set the dates to the last 28 days, and sort by impressions.
3. Send me the top 10 products. I'll sharpen the titles and descriptions of those first, plus the framed prints, multi
   frames, blankets and cushions whatever they show.

**I'll do (after Steps 1–4):** feed and product-page fixes, in one small pull request for you to approve.

**Your decision:** a free card **with every framed print** can be shown as a promotion badge on Google. The current "free
first card" can't, because Google rejects first-order-only offers
([S](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13422697?hl=en-GB)). It costs about **£1.90 an order**. If you say yes, I'll
write both the site change and the promotion wording.

## Part 2: the £100 Google Shopping test (Google Ads, about 1 hour, account by Fri 27 Nov, live Wed 2 Dec)

**What it does:** shows the framed prints, multi frames, blankets and cushions to people in the UK who search for gifts like
them. You pay only when someone clicks, at **no more than £0.70 a click**, capped at **£7 a day**, from **2 to 14 Dec**: about
£90 in total.

### A. Open the Google Ads account (Fri 27 Nov at the latest)

1. Go to [ads.google.com](https://ads.google.com/) and click **Start now**. Use the same Google login as Merchant Center.
2. Google will try to walk you into a quick "smart" campaign. Look for the small link **Switch to Expert Mode**, or **Create
   an account without a campaign**, and click it. **Don't let it create a campaign yet.**
3. Fill in the billing country (United Kingdom), time zone (London) and currency (**GBP**; this can't be changed later),
   then add the payment card. The card is yours to enter; I can't do this step.
4. If Google asks for **advertiser verification**, start it straight away. It can take several days, which is why this
   step is a week early.
5. If Google offers to install a **Google tag** or **conversion tracking**, choose **skip / do it later**. The site stays
   tracker-free.

### B. Link it to Merchant Center

1. In **Merchant Center**, click the **gear icon**, then **Apps and services** (it may say **Linked accounts**), then
   **Google Ads**.
2. Choose **Link account** and pick (or type) the new Google Ads account's 10-digit customer ID. It's shown at the top right
   of Google Ads.
3. In **Google Ads**, go to **Tools**, then **Data manager** (or **Linked accounts**), then **Google Merchant Center**, and
   **Approve** the request.

### C. Turn off automatic changes

1. In Google Ads, open **Recommendations**, then **Auto-apply**.
2. **Untick everything.** Otherwise Google can raise budgets or add campaigns on its own.

### D. Build the campaign (Tue 1 Dec, set to start Wed 2 Dec)

These follow Google's own steps for a Shopping campaign ([S](https://support.google.com/adwords/answer/2456103?hl=en-gb)).

1. Click the **+** button, then **New campaign**.
2. For the objective, choose **Create a campaign without a goal's guidance**. That avoids the screens that ask for
   conversion tracking.
3. Campaign type: **Shopping**. Not Performance Max, which may hold back spend without tracking
   ([S](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10724896?hl=en)).
4. Choose the **Snickerdoodle Merchant Center** account. This can't be changed later.
5. Campaign name: **Xmas 26: frames, blankets, cushions**.
6. **Budget and bidding:**
   - Bidding: **Maximise clicks**, and tick **Set a maximum cost per click bid limit**: **£0.70**. If it only offers
     **Manual CPC**, choose that and set the ad group bid to £0.60.
   - Daily budget: **£7**.
   - Customer acquisition: leave as it is.
   - Campaign priority: leave as **Low**.
7. **Campaign settings:**
   - **Networks:** untick **Google search partners**, so only Google itself shows the ads.
   - **Locations:** **United Kingdom**. Under **Location options**, choose **Presence: people in or regularly in** the UK.
   - **More settings:** start date **Wed 2 Dec**, end date **Mon 14 Dec**.
8. **Ad group:** name it **Frames blankets cushions**.
9. **Product groups.** This is the step that keeps it to the products that leave the most:
   1. Click the pencil next to **All products**, choose **subdivide by Product type**, level 1, and tick **Home**.
   2. Open **Home** and subdivide again by **Product type**, level 2. Tick **Framed prints** (this includes the multi frames),
      **Blankets** and **Cushions**.
   3. Set **everything else**, at both levels, to **Excluded**.
   
   These names come straight from our feed (`Home > Framed prints`, `Home > Blankets`, `Home > Cushions`).
10. Click **Next**, check the summary says £7 a day, UK, 2–14 Dec, then **Publish campaign**.

### E. Words to keep out (5 minutes, after publishing)

1. Open the campaign, then **Keywords**, then **Negative keywords**, then **+**.
2. Add: **free**, **printable**, **template**, **diy**, **how to**, **cheap**, **wholesale**, **jobs**. These stop paying for
   searches that won't buy a £60 keepsake.

### F. Each Monday (7 Dec and 14 Dec)

1. In Google Ads, open the campaign and note **Clicks**, **Cost** and **Average CPC**.
2. Open **Insights and reports**, then **Search terms**, and add any silly ones as negative keywords.
3. Send me the three numbers. I'll set them against the frame, blanket and cushion orders for 2–14 Dec, compared with the
   fortnight before. That's our rough read, because there's no tag on the site.

**Stop rules:**
- **Average CPC above £0.90 after 3 days:** lower the limit to £0.50.
- **No orders at all by Mon 7 Dec:** pause it. Its £50 has bought the lesson.
- **Mon 14 Dec:** it ends by itself.

## What this costs and what it won't do

- **Cost:** Part 1 is free. Part 2 is about £90, never more than £7 a day.
- **What it won't do:** there's no store star rating before Christmas, because Google needs about 100 reviews
  ([S](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/190657)). There's no exact sales count from the ads, because there's no
  tag. And there's no audience based on anyone's children: it shows on search words only.
