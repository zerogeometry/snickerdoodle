# Privacy notice — proposed wording for the family calendar

> Draft for the founder's sign-off · 27 Oct 2026 · to add to `/trust/privacy`
> (`src/app/(marketing)/trust/privacy/page.tsx`). See `DPIA-addendum-family-calendar.md`.

## Under "What we collect" — add

- **Your family calendar** — the occasions you or your family add (a name and a date, and if you like,
  who it's for and whether it follows the lunar calendar), and the days your family chooses to celebrate.

> **About the days you celebrate.** Some of these choices — like religious festivals, or Lunar New Year —
> could say something about your family's beliefs or background. So we treat them with extra care: we
> only use them to put those days on your family's calendar and in the reminders you've asked for. We
> never use them for marketing, never use them to decide what we show or offer you, and never share them
> outside your family. You can change them, or untick them all, any time on your Family page.

## Under "How we use it" — add

- **To keep your family's calendar** — showing the dates you've added, the days you celebrate and your
  little ones' birthdays to the people you've shared the calendar with, and only at the circle you chose.
  *Legal basis: to provide the service you asked for; for the days you celebrate, your explicit consent
  (you tick them yourself, and can untick them any time).*

## Change to the existing reminders line

> **To send you birthday and occasion reminders** — a month before and two weeks before each one, only
> if you've turned them on (everyone in your family chooses their own). *Legal basis: your consent.* You
> can turn them off in one tap, any time.

## Under "Who can see what" — add

- Dates on your family calendar are seen only by people in your family you've shared the calendar with —
  just the name and the date, never a picture. An occasion can be about someone who isn't on
  Snickerdoodle (like a grandparent's anniversary); we only keep the name and the date.
