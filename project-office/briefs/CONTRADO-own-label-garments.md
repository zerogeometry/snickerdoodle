# Contrado own-label garments — cost comparison vs Prodigi

> **DEFERRED (founder decision, 2026-07-07):** the Contrado all-over-print garment line is OFF
> the plan for now — launch garments are Prodigi DTG front-prints presented as standard flat
> product photos. Revisit only when the preview presentation earns it.

> Prepared 2026-07-03 for the branding decision: Prodigi garments inherit the blank-maker's neck
> label (Stanley/Stella, Bella+Canvas, Rabbit Skins); Contrado makes garments cut-and-sew in
> London from printed fabric, so the label can be OURS. This is the cost of that label.
> All Contrado prices captured live from contrado.co.uk on 2026-07-03. Inc-VAT unless stated.

## 1. What "own label" actually gets you at Contrado
- **Sewn-in custom label** — explicitly offered on the Kids Premium T-Shirt ("add a custom label
  that will be sewn inside the t-shirt"); their cut-and-sew model means no third-party branding
  anywhere on the garment.
- **Dropship & White Label programme** ("full branding, no inventory, auto-fulfilment") —
  Shopify-app-first; API/manual for us. Contrado-neutral packaging + own-label dispatch note.
- They also SELL the branding hardware as products: fabric labels, swing tags (card + leather +
  folded), custom box sleeves, card box packaging, luxury custom boxes — useful for any future
  self-fulfilled or bundled tier.
- UK-made (Park Royal, London), ships in 1–2 days; standard UK delivery is Royal Mail 1st Class
  **Recorded** (94% next-day) — effectively everything is tracked. Tee-size premium tracked £5.99.

## 2. The discount structure (verified)
- Public wholesale calculator ("Quick Peek", no account): **flat 20% off retail** — tested at
  qty 1, 5, 25, 100; it does not tier up publicly.
- Marketing: "save up to 40%". The featured wholesale tiles ARE 40% off retail (Mens T-Shirt
  wholesale £15.57 vs £25.95 retail), so 40% exists — but it's account-gated (volume /
  key-account). **Assume 20% at our launch volumes; 40% is the earn-into rate.**
- ⚠️ Confirm on trade-account signup (already a founder action in the keepsake-box brief):
  the dropship app's actual rate, whether trade prices include delivery, and the volume
  thresholds for the deeper tiers.

## 3. Head-to-head (garments)

"Required retail" = founder formula: exc-VAT price = (cost inc VAT + Stripe fees) × 1.25 +
shipping inc VAT, then VAT on top, charm-rounded. Contrado ship assumed £5.99.

Readings:
- **Kids tee**: own label costs +£12 on the shelf at the 20% tier (£33.95 vs £20.95) — a 62%
  price rise on the flagship kids garment. Even at the 40% tier it's +£6 (~£26.95).
- **Adult tees**: at the 40% tier Contrado is roughly **cost-parity with Prodigi's womens
  Bella+Canvas** (£20.96 vs up to £23.34 all-in) — required retail ~£29.95–30.95 vs £28.95
  today. This is the one garment where own-label nearly pays for itself, once volume unlocks 40%.
- **Bodysuit**: the new-baby gift anchor cannot move — stays Prodigi (Rabbit Skins RS-4424).

## 4. Product-quality trade (not just price)
- Contrado kids tees are **poly "Soft Fashion Jersey"** (dye-sub, all-over print capable, vivid).
  Prodigi's STTK909 is **Stanley/Stella organic cotton**. For a children's keepsake brand,
  "organic cotton" is arguably the stronger product story even with someone else's size label;
  poly is the stronger print with our label. Adult range does have cotton options at Contrado.
- A sewn-in label whose garment reads "polyester" may cost more brand equity with parents than
  a Stanley/Stella label costs us. Worth ordering samples of both before deciding.

## 5. Strategic recommendation (household-name ambition)
The label is not where the brand is won at launch volumes — the economics say so (+38–62% shelf
price at the accessible tier). Recommended ladder:
1. **Launch on Prodigi economics.** Own the brand at the moments we control: the app, the
   keepsake home, packaging inserts (verify Prodigi's branding/insert options), the gift loop.
2. **Introduce Contrado selectively as a "signature" tier** where the product is genuinely
   better/exclusive and carries a premium price honestly: the keepsake memory box (see
   [CONTRADO-keepsake-box.md](CONTRADO-keepsake-box.md)), possibly an all-over-print premium
   kids tee with the sewn-in label at ~£27.95–29.95 positioned as the premium garment.
3. **Earn into the 40% tier / revisit at volume** — at 40%, adult tees flip to parity and the
   whole own-label range becomes rational. Beyond that: private-label manufacturing.
- Same compliance gate as the box: Contrado = a NEW processor of children's images. Article 28
  DPA + no-retention + residency checklist (§5 of the keepsake-box brief) before any live image.

## 6. Hero item: matching family set — the cotton constraint (verified 2026-07-03)
Founder direction: hero = matching garments for girls, boys AND adults; fabric = organic cotton,
or at least cotton — poly only if no cotton path exists.

What Contrado can and cannot do in cotton:
- **Adults: YES — all-over print on real cotton.** Men's Premium Cotton T-Shirt: Supima cotton
  jersey, REACTIVE print ("embedded deep into the fibres"), "your design printed all-over",
  from £25.95 (was £29), 93 reviews, sizes 2XS–7XL, compostable packaging. Women's Premium
  Cotton T-Shirt: same all-over reactive cotton, from £24.95 (was £29), 55 reviews, "various
  jersey fabric options available" (an ORGANIC cotton jersey option may exist in the design
  tool — Contrado prints organic cotton fabric; confirm on trade account). NOTE: these pages
  banner wholesale "up to 30%" (vs 20% on kids pages) — per-product caps vary.
- **Kids (3–14): NO cotton exists.** Contrado's entire kids garment range is poly Soft Fashion
  Jersey (basic tee £21.45, premium tee £22.95, leggings, flip flops). A Contrado kids garment
  breaks the fabric rule, full stop.
- **Baby: no garments.** Closest is the baby bib, from £18, "soft poly & cotton towelling".

Consequence: **the child — the centre of the brand — is the one family member Contrado cannot
dress in cotton.** So the matching family set does NOT launch on Contrado:
1. **Launch the set on Prodigi organic cotton (available today):** kids STTK909 (Stanley/Stella
   "Mini Creator", organic) + mens STTM559 (S/S "Creator", organic — literally the same garment
   design as the kids tee in adult cut: a true matching story) + womens BC-6004 (Airlume cotton;
   CHECK Prodigi for a womens Stanley/Stella equivalent so the whole family wears the same
   organic garment) + baby RS-4424 bodysuit (cotton) — the baby is IN the set, which Contrado
   can never offer. Same placement print of the drawing on every chest. Implement as a bundle
   flow (one drawing → add the whole set), not new SKUs; consider a small set saving.
2. **Contrado enters as the premium ADULT all-over piece** ("the grown-up keepsake tee",
   Supima all-over, ~£33–36 at entry trade / ~£31 at 30%) — an upsell inside the set flow,
   not a replacement for it.
3. **Kids all-over cotton = future gap.** Revisit if Contrado adds cotton kidswear or another
   UK cut-and-sew printer offers organic-cotton kids garments with a DPA.
