# Personalised children's books — competitive scan (2026-07-10)

> Deep-research pass (web fan-out → adversarial 3-vote verification → cited synthesis). Confidence
> is marked per point; unverified/uncertain items are flagged honestly. Sources inline.

## Headline
The model we're proposing — **human-authored/illustrated stories + name/preset-avatar personalisation
+ zero-inventory global print-on-demand** — is not a bet. It's the **proven, category-winning model**,
and the incumbent that runs it just **exited to Penguin Random House for ~€99m**. The AI-likeness
version we'd layer on (v2) is a *separate, newer* camp that nobody has yet combined with premium
human craft **or** with a strong (no-retention / in-region) child-photo privacy posture. That gap is
our opening.

## 1. The craft incumbents (our v1 model, validated)
- **Wonderbly** (ex-"Lost My Name") — the category leader. **11M+ books sold across 140+ countries**;
  flagship "The Little Boy/Girl Who Lost His Name" ~4M lifetime. Personalisation = **name + a
  selectable illustrated avatar** (skin tone, hair, eyes, glasses, freckles) — **no photo, no AI
  likeness**. Stories/art **human-authored and illustrated**. **DTC + worldwide on-demand printers,
  zero inventory, high margin.** Funding arc: Dragon's Den seed → $9M Series A (Google Ventures, 2015)
  → $8.5M Series B (Ravensburger, 2017) → Graphite Capital PE buy-in (2021) → **sold to Penguin
  Random House, June 2025** (~€99m; grown to £60m+ sales / £10m+ EBITDA). [high confidence]
  Sources: graphitecapital.com/portfolio/wonderbly, global.penguinrandomhouse.com (June 2025
  acquisition announcement), techcrunch.com/2017/07/31/wonderbly, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonderbly,
  wonderbly.com/uk.
- **Librio** (Swiss, ~2017) — second craft player, same shape: **name + selectable avatar (750+
  combos incl. skin, hair, glasses, hearing aid)**, human-written/hand-illustrated, craft +
  sustainability branding (recycled paper, tree-planting). **No photo/AI.** [high confidence]
  Source: uk.librio.com.
- **Not verified this pass:** Put Me In The Story, I See Me!, Dinkleboo (no surviving claims), and
  Hooray Heroes (its model + a rumoured ~£46.99 price were **refuted/unconfirmed** — don't rely on
  either). Gap to close later.

**Read:** every credible craft incumbent personalises by **name + preset avatar, never a photo/AI
likeness**, and the biggest one is a nine-figure exit. Our v1 is the safe, proven core.

## 2. The AI-likeness wave (our v2 territory)
A distinct, newer camp does the **photo → AI-stylised child inserted as the character** thing the
incumbents avoid — but by **routing the child's photo through third-party US foundation models**:
- **OnceUponBooks** — processes child photos via **OpenAI + Google Gemini**; deletes originals within
  **30 days**; "not used to train AI models." [high confidence] Source: onceuponbooks.com/privacy.
- **Childbook.ai** — photo → AI illustration; deletes photos **max 14 days *after subscription ends***
  (clock starts late), **keeps the AI illustrations indefinitely**; policy dated **Dec 2022** (stale).
  [high confidence] Source: childbook.ai/privacy-policy.
- **Magic Story** — *unverified extract, worth watching:* claims **human-written story + AI
  illustration from a photo** (a partial hybrid). Not verified — treat as a lead. Source:
  magicstory.com/blog.

**Not established by this pass (important):** the specific **quality failures** of these AI products —
likeness fidelity, **cross-page character consistency**, uncanny-valley — did **NOT** survive
verification. There's general press backlash against AI kids' books (e.g. TIME on "Alice and
Sparkle"; NCSU, Macquarie, Canadian School Libraries critiques of AI picture-book quality/ethics),
but **no verified teardown** proving the likeness/consistency weakness we assumed. So "AI competitors
render the child inconsistently" is a **plausible prior, not a verified fact** — confirm before we
lean on it. (Open question flagged by the research.)

## 3. Child-photo privacy — our genuine edge
The AI camp's posture is **retain-then-delete (14–30 days) via third-party US models**, no-training
*self-stated* and unaudited, and dependent on OpenAI/Gemini whose own retention/training terms are
separate. **None offers true no-retention, no-third-party-exposure, or in-region processing.**
Industry best-practice guidance is trending the *other* way — documented immediate deletion +
no-training-by-default (ICO-style). Our constitution's posture (**no-retention, no-training,
single-image, in-region, consent-first**) is therefore **materially stronger than the AI incumbents'**
— a real, marketable differentiator, especially for a *children's* product. [high confidence]
Sources: onceuponbooks.com/privacy, childbook.ai/privacy-policy, legalnodes.com (child-data AI
compliance).

## 4. Pricing (UK £)
- **Verified:** Librio standard personalised **hardcover = £24.99**. Against our **~£8 POD base
  cost**, that's a healthy gross margin and puts the craft-personalised segment firmly in the
  **~£20–25** band. Source: uk.librio.com. [high confidence]
- **Not verbatim-verified:** exact Wonderbly per-book prices and the AI startups' pricing (my prior:
  Wonderbly hardback ~£25–30, deluxe higher; AI startups often lower / subscription — **unconfirmed**).

## 5. White space (medium confidence — an inference, not a sourced claim)
The market is **cleanly bifurcated**: craft incumbents (human craft, preset avatar, *no* AI) vs AI
startups (photo→likeness via third-party models, weak privacy, *not* positioned on premium craft).
**No verified player credibly pairs premium human craft + a careful, privacy-safe AI child-likeness.**
That combined position looks **open** — which is exactly the v1→v2 ladder we discussed. Caveat: not an
exhaustive hybrid sweep; Magic Story is a possible partial exception; recent 2025–26 entrants weren't
fully covered.

## Recommendation (sharpened by the data)
1. **v1 = the proven craft model:** ~5 in-house-written/illustrated stories + **name + preset avatar**
   (steal Librio's inclusivity depth — skin, hair, glasses, hearing aid). POD hardback. Price
   **~£22–25** (at/just under Librio; ~£14–17 gross margin on ~£8 cost). Ships without touching the
   AI/safety gate. This is the whole business Wonderbly proved.
2. **v2 = AI child-likeness as the *premium* tier — led by privacy, not novelty.** Our angle isn't
   "we do AI too"; it's "**your child, drawn into a beautiful book — processed in-region, single-image,
   never retained, never used to train.**" That's the one thing the AI incumbents structurally can't
   copy, and the craft incumbents won't. Phase-3, preset-only, output re-screened per our constitution.
3. **Before committing v2, verify the quality gap** (the open question): a targeted teardown of
   OnceUponBooks / Childbook.ai / Magic Story et al. — likeness fidelity + cross-page consistency +
   uncanny valley — via reviews (Trustpilot/Reddit/app stores) and journalist critiques, so we know
   exactly where they fail and how high our bar must be.

## Honest caveats
Wonderbly scale/financials are company/PE-reported (cross-corroborated by PRH/Bertelsmann + trade
press, but unaudited); the 11M spans some *adult* titles too. All privacy claims are self-stated
policy, not verified behaviour. The AI-quality-failure question is **unresolved**. Several competitors
(Hooray Heroes, Put Me In The Story, I See Me!, Dinkleboo) yielded no verified data. The white-space
conclusion is a reasoned inference, not a directly sourced fact.
