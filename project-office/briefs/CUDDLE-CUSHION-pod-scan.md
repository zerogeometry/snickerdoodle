# Cuddle cushion — UK POD supplier scan (2026-07-10)

> Deep-research pass (web fan-out → adversarial 3-vote verification → cited synthesis) for a
> **custom CUT-TO-ARBITRARY-OUTLINE** soft cushion from a child's drawing ("the drawing made
> huggable"). Confidence marked per point; sources inline. Prices/MOQ/API are snapshots — re-check
> before committing.

## The headline (the fork)
**No single UK supplier offers BOTH an arbitrary cut-to-outline soft cushion AND Prodigi-grade
automated API/blind-ship dropship.** You choose one axis to lead on:
- **Product magic** (true cut-to-outline) → **HappySnapGifts**, but automation is unproven (likely
  manual per-order at first).
- **Automation** (blind-ship / API) → **Pictures to Fabric** or **Contrado**, but **preset shapes
  only** — which loses the whole "hug your drawing" point.

## The shortlist

### 1. HappySnapGifts (Clear Prospects Ltd, Blackburn, Lancs) — BEST PRODUCT FIT
- **Genuinely cuts to an arbitrary outline:** "Printed and then hand cut to shaped outline",
  "Face & Body shape from your own photo." Preset square/round cushions are *separate* SKUs, so this
  is truly bespoke-outline, not preset. [high]
- **UK-made, fast, no MOQ:** made to order in **1–2 days**, qty 1, in **soft velvet/mock-suede**,
  two sizes (~20–30cm and XL ~40–50cm), **from £14.99**. [high]
- **Already on the "cushion" safety side:** certified to the **British Standard Furniture &
  Furnishings (Fire) Safety Regs** (furnishing route, not toy). [high]
- **The catch — automation is unproven:** it's a **consumer retailer**; no API / feed / blind-ship /
  reseller programme was found. This is *absence of evidence*, not a confirmed "no" — **needs a
  direct call**. [medium]
- **Mild extrapolation:** the product is oriented to *photos of faces/pets/people*, so cutting to a
  child's **line drawing** needs a **test order** to prove quality (and that they'll cut where we
  want). *Bonus:* because **they** do the cutting, our own background-removal doesn't have to be
  pixel-perfect — we send the artwork + a suggested cut-line, they cut the fabric.
- Sources: happysnapgifts.co.uk/products/photo-cushion-hand-cut-shape, clearprospects.com

### 2. Pictures to Fabric (Wetherby, W. Yorks) — BEST AUTOMATION, WRONG SHAPE
- **Only confirmed UK blind-ship/white-label cushion dropshipper:** dye-sublimation, **single unit,
  no MOQ**, ships "branded as if it came directly from you… with your sender's address." [high]
- **But:** cushions are **PRESET shapes only** (square/oblong/floor/collage) — **no cut-to-outline**
  — and ordering is **manual web upload, no API** ("after you've uploaded your order… there's
  nothing more for you to do"). So: blind-drop, but manual + preset. [high]
- Source: picturestofabric.co.uk/dropshipping

### 3. Contrado (London / Park Royal) — API + UK + SOFT, WRONG SHAPE
- Made-to-order **MOQ 1**, all printing/production/fulfilment **in-house UK**, true soft fabrics
  (velvet, faux suede), and a **known API/dropship POD** — **but cushions are PRESET shapes only**
  (square/rectangle/round/heart/bolster); **no upload-to-cut-outline**. [high]
- **Why it still matters:** it's the one player that already has *API + UK + soft fabric* — so it's
  the top candidate for a **"would you add a bespoke cut-to-shape SKU?"** enquiry (see open
  questions). Source: contrado.co.uk/printed-cushions

### 4. Budsies "Buddy Pillows" — PROVES THE PRODUCT, WRONG GEOGRAPHY
- Genuine **cut-to-shape plush pillow from an uploaded image** (full/half/head crops, double-sided,
  **$49–69**, no MOQ) — but **US-based, made overseas (China), 2–8 week** turnaround, **no
  API/white-label**. Fails UK-sourcing + low-ops + speed; useful as a product/quality benchmark.
  [high] Source: budsies.com/custom-pillows

### 5. Drawing-→-plush TOY makers (the *parked hero*, not this) — for reference
- **Maua Gang** (UK, handmade, UKCA toy-marked, age 0+, **£40–220**, 8–12wk, wholesale MOQ ~10) and
  **Picture to Puppet** (Edinburgh, MOQ 1, **£120–400+**, manual quote, no API). Confirms the sculpted
  plush **toy** is a distinct, premium (**£120–400+**), non-API, slow category — exactly why we
  parked it. [high]

## Safety & positioning [medium]
Observed posture: **cushion** suppliers certify to **furnishing fire-safety** regs; **toy** makers
carry **UKCA toy marking (EN71)**. So marketing ours as a **decorative "cuddle cushion" (a keepsake),
not a baby toy**, keeps us on the lighter furnishing-fire-safety side. This is inferred from how
suppliers self-position, **not** a definitive regulatory ruling — given our child-focused brand, get
a **compliance opinion** on the cushion-vs-toy line and labelling. (Our CLAUDE.md already flags this.)

## Pricing
Base ~**£15** (HappySnapGifts hand-cut). Suggested **retail ~£28–35** — a warm, giftable keepsake
comfortably inside our £18–65 band, at a healthy margin. (Vs plush **toys** £120–400.)

3. **Would Contrado (or Pictures to Fabric) add a bespoke cut-to-shape SKU?** — that would be the
   ideal *API + UK + magic* combo this scan couldn't find off-the-shelf.
4. **Definitive UK toy-safety line** (EN71/UKCA/CE, flammability, small-parts) for a cut-to-shape
   cushion plausibly used by young children — specialist opinion.
