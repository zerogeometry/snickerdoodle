# 01 — Product Requirements

## North star

Childhood is fleeting and most of what a child makes gets lost. Snickerdoodle exists to **keep the magic** — turning throwaway kids' art into kept, beautiful keepsakes, inside a private family space that grows with the child for ~18 years. Signature tension: **we frame the wonky bits in gold** (premium craft + celebrated imperfection).

## What the product is

A UK children-and-families personalised gifting brand (snickerdoodle.co.uk). Parents turn a child's drawings into keepsakes and gifts — cards, prints, mugs, tees, a personalised storybook, 40+ products — held in a private **Family Keepsake Home** that the whole (and extended) family can be invited into.

- **Wedge:** kids' drawings → cards & gifts.
- **Flagship:** personalised storybook (high AOV).
- **Retention:** the Keepsake Home + a gentle occasion/reminder engine + the family circle.

## The three engines (the product must serve all three)

1. **Creation** — a drawing becomes a whole family range, in moments.
2. **Acquisition** — a gift list / pre-filled link → relatives buy → some join the family. One child, many wallets.
3. **Retention** — the Keepsake Home (an 18-year relationship), the occasion engine, the family graph.

## The moat (design decisions must protect it)

Incumbents (Moonpig, Wonderbly) are transactional. Snickerdoodle is **relational** — an integrated family ecosystem where one input becomes many products, the family graph compounds, and **trust is demonstrated through control**. That graph is the defensible asset.

## Goals & objectives (by horizon)

- **Launch (MVP):** prove the wedge — upload a drawing, see it on a range, create and buy a product, keep it privately. Trust pages live; compliance gates passed.
- **Early growth:** activate the family loop — gift lists, pre-filled links, invite-to-family + email verification, join-&-save, the occasion engine.
- **Scale:** the storybook flagship at quality/AOV; catalogue breadth that makes "one drawing → 40+ products" feel astonishing; the reminder engine as durable retention.
- **Later (out of scope now):** vertical 2 (personalised workwear) on the same engine.

## Success metrics

- **Activation:** % who upload a drawing; % who reach the "made real" range view; % who create a first product.
- **Conversion:** upload → first paid order; gift-link open → purchase.
- **Family-graph growth:** invites sent/accepted; avg. circle size per family; % of buyers who join a family.
- **AOV & attach:** average order value; add-on attach rate; storybook revenue share.
- **Retention:** repeat purchase; occasion-nudge → purchase; keepsakes kept per child over time.
- **Trust health:** privacy/sharing support tickets; refunds driven by print quality (target low — gated by the DPI/upscale requirement).

## Users

- **Primary:** parents of young children (UK), mobile-first, time-poor, sentimental, privacy-aware.
- **Secondary (buyers/joiners):** grandparents and relatives — often less tech-confident, usually on a phone, arriving via a gift link. The guest-checkout and join-family flows must be effortless for *them*.

## Scope & non-goals

**In scope (MVP → early growth):** drawings → products; the Studio (magic edits + print-readiness); catalogue & product application; cart, guest + member checkout, add-ons; the Family Keepsake Home with per-member sections & per-asset visibility; invites & the family circle; gift lists & pre-filled links; join-&-save; the occasion engine; public trust pages.

**Explicit non-goals (do not build):**
- **No open marketplace** (user-to-user selling) — child-safety/moderation risk.
- **No public profiles / social feed** — the Keepsake Home is a private box, not a social page.
- **No workwear / vertical 2 yet.**
- **No raw "AI" surfaces** — magic edits only.
- **No dark-pattern upsell** — add-ons stay small and tasteful; guest checkout stays one tap away.

## Definition of launch-ready

A parent can upload a drawing, make and keep a keepsake privately, buy a product that prints at quality, share a pre-filled gift link, have a grandparent join the family and buy from a phone, and begin receiving gentle occasion nudges — with every sharing decision under the parent's control, nothing public, AI invisible and non-retaining, and all compliance launch gates closed (see `docs/05`).
