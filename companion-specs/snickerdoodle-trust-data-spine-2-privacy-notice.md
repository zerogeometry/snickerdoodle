## The short version

Everything you make on Snickerdoodle is yours. We use your child's drawings and photos to make the things you order, we don't sell your data, we don't let anyone train AI on it, and we delete what we don't need. You can see and delete anything, any time. The full detail is below.

---

## 1. Who we are

Snickerdoodle is a service operated by **Launch Mode Ltd** ([company number], registered in England & Wales, registered office [address]). We are the "data controller" for the personal information described here. You can reach us about anything in this notice at **[privacy@snickerdoodle.co.uk]**.

We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), registration number **[ICO number]**.

## 2. The information we collect

- **Your account details** — your name, email, and a securely hashed password.
- **Your child's details** — a first name and birthday/age, if you choose to add them (for personalising products and, if you opt in, birthday reminders).
- **Your creations** — the drawings and photos you upload, and the designs we make from them.
- **Your orders** — what you bought, your delivery address, and a secure payment token (we never see or store your full card details — those go straight to our payment provider).
- **Your preferences** — your marketing and reminder choices.
- **Wishlists and shares** — the wishlists you create and the private links you share with family.
- **Basic technical data** — things like your device and cookie data, for security and to keep the site working.

## 3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

- **To make and deliver what you order** — including processing your child's drawing or photo into the product. *Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.*
- **To run your account and your Keepsake Home.** *Legal basis: contract.*
- **To send you marketing or birthday reminders** — only if you've opted in. *Legal basis: your consent.* You can withdraw it any time.
- **To keep the service secure and prevent fraud.** *Legal basis: our legitimate interests.*
- **To meet legal obligations** — for example, keeping order records for tax. *Legal basis: legal obligation.*

## 4. Children's information — how we look after it

Snickerdoodle is for grown-ups to use, but it's built entirely around children's drawings and photos, so we hold children's data to a higher standard than the law's minimum. We have chosen to design the service in line with the ICO's Children's Code. In practice that means:

- **Privacy is the default.** Nothing about your child is ever public unless you actively choose to share it.
- **We collect as little as possible** — only what's needed to make your product.
- **We never profile children** and we never use children's data for behavioural advertising.
- **You're in control.** You can see and delete anything about your child, any time, in one place.

## 5. Artificial intelligence, in plain terms

We use AI to turn drawings and photos into beautiful products. When we do:

- Your image is used **only** to create the design you asked for.
- Our AI providers are **contractually forbidden from keeping your images or using them to train any AI model.**
- We don't make any decision about you by automated means that has a legal or similarly significant effect.

## 6. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We share it only with the trusted suppliers who help us run the service — our hosting, AI design, print-and-delivery, payment, and messaging providers — and only so they can do their job for us. Each is bound by a contract that limits them to our instructions and protects your data. We never share your data with other Snickerdoodle families.

## 7. Sending data outside the UK

Some of our suppliers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we make sure your data is protected by an approved safeguard (such as the UK's international data transfer agreement or a recognised data bridge), and we assess that the protection is not materially lower than UK standards — with extra care for children's data.

## 8. How long we keep it

- **Drawings and photos you upload:** only as long as we need them to make your order, plus a short reprint window — then we delete them, **unless** you've chosen to keep them in your Keepsake Home.
- **Keepsake Home items:** for as long as you want them. Delete any time.
- **Order records:** six years, because tax law requires it.
- **Account data:** until you close your account.
- **Marketing choices:** until you change them.

## 9. Your rights

You can ask us to: give you a copy of your data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to how we use it; or give it to you in a portable form. You can withdraw consent (for marketing or reminders) at any time. We'll respond within the legal timeframe — though we may pause the clock if we need to confirm your identity or clarify your request.

## 10. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can **complain directly to us** at **[privacy@snickerdoodle.co.uk]** or using our complaint form at **[link]** — we'll acknowledge it within 30 days and keep you posted. You also have the right to complain to the **Information Commissioner's Office** at ico.org.uk, though we'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

## 11. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to keep the site secure and working, and (only with your agreement) to understand how it's used. You can manage non-essential cookies any time via our cookie settings.

## 12. Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your data, we'll update this notice and, where it matters, tell you directly.
