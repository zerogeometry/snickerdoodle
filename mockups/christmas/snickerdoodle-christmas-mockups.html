Snickerdoodle — Christmas UI mockups

Five concept screens for the Christmas season (1 Nov → 6 Jan). They layer stickers, tape and copy onto the existing scrapbook look, as decided in D12, and add no new colours. The tokens are the same as globals.css. The festive washi tape mixes tomato, leaf and paper, and the gold is honey. Photos are concept images generated locally with Qwen, standing in for real product mockups. They don't show any real child's art or real product renders. Delivery dates are placeholders until Prodigi publishes its 2026 cut-offs.

Rules these mockups keep

  • Tokens only. The Christmas look recombines tomato, leaf, honey, sky and paper. There's no red/green palette swap and no new hex values.
  • Motion: snow and the sticker wiggle are CSS-only and transform/opacity-only. Both are off under prefers-reduced-motion. They're never on an ancestor of the tab bar or sheets.
  • One Gaegu moment per view. Primary actions sit in the thumb zone. Every tap target is 44px or more.
  • Trust: no public gallery and no "share your drawing" competition. The gift link is parent-initiated, with circle and access shown. First name only on the bauble.
  • No "AI" anywhere in the copy. Festive touches are deterministic presets.
  • Bundle price in screen 5 is illustrative. Bauble £21 + mug £18 = £39. The real saving on a same-lab parcel is about £3.30 (4.30 + 4.30 − 5.30), so the bundle can honestly be £1–2 off.
