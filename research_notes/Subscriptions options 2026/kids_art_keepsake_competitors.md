# Subscription and membership plans: kids' art keepsake services, family memory apps and kids' creative apps (UK focus, researched 26 Nov 2026)

Notes on method: prices come from official pricing pages and UK App Store (apps.apple.com/gb) in-app purchase lists, fetched on 26 Nov 2026 unless another date is given. The UK App Store lists IAP prices but often does not say which price is monthly and which is annual, so where a period is not labelled it is flagged as "period not labelled". USD to GBP conversions are indicative only, at roughly £0.75–£0.80 per $1. No live FX rate was checked.

## 1. Plans and prices, competitor by competitor (names, monthly vs annual, free tier, what paid unlocks)

### Takeaway
Pure kids' art apps cluster at about £3–£12 a month, or £30–£145 a year. Most use "unlimited storage" as the hook and bundle book discounts or credits. Family memory apps use a freemium cap instead (Tinybeans 20 uploads a month and 5GB; Keepy 20 keepies a month in the UK) and charge about £5.50–£8 a month or £55–£70 a year. The UK players that turn kids' art into books (Doodle Nest, Silk Giraffe, Cards to Books, Point101, MadeForMemory, Inkifi) all appear to be one-off purchases. None was found selling a subscription.

**Apple rules relevant to a kids' app subscription**
- Kids Category (1.3):
  - Apps must not include links out, purchasing opportunities or other distractions "unless reserved for a designated area behind a parental gate".
  - No third-party analytics or advertising, and no PII or device info sent to third parties.
  - 5.1.4: a parental gate is "generally not the same as securing parental consent".
  - Source: [Apple App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- Subscriptions (3.1.2): at least 7 days long, available across all of the user's devices, and must "clearly describe what the user will get for the price". — [Apple App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)

### Inferences
- UK parents already accept about **£6.99/month or £40–£60/year** for a kids' creative app (Sago, Pok Pok, DUPLO). Family archive apps sit at about **£5.49–£7.99/month or £55–£70/year**. A Snickerdoodle membership around £3–£5/month or £30–£50/year would sit below both, unless it bundles physical product.
- Artkive's structure (VIP = boxes; DIY tiers = storage + % off books + a free book) is the clearest template for bundling. Note that Artkive bills annually only.
- UK art-book players price **per book, per number of pieces** (£89–£195+), and none was found to offer a membership. That leaves a "UK keepsake membership" largely uncontested.
- Lifetime tiers exist (Keepy £99.99, Pok Pok £89.99). They suit a "they're only little once" framing, but they create a perpetual storage liability.

## 2. Bundling physical product into the subscription

### Takeaway
Some bundling exists, but it is thin. Artkive bundles a free book a year (Creator, Book Lover) or 2 mail-in boxes a year (VIP). Notabli has auto-printed "Book subscriptions". Tinybeans+ and FamilyAlbum Premium bundle only **free photobook shipping**. No one bundles monthly prints, sticker sheets or gifts.

### Cited Findings
- Artkive Creator includes a free 25-page DIY hardcover ($30 value) and Book Lover a free 95-page hardcover ($100 value), each with 20–30% off further books. VIP includes 2 free Artkive Boxes a year plus free rush. — [Artkive plans](https://artkiveapp.com/plans)
- Artkive credits are re-issued each annual billing period and expire 12 months after issue (search snippet). — [Artkive FAQ: membership plans](https://faq.artkiveapp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038276252-All-About-Membership-Plans)
- Notabli "can optionally print hardcover photo books... automatically with Notabli Book subscriptions". — [Notabli App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/notabli/id580644870)
- Tinybeans+ includes free shipping on photo books. — [Tinybeans help: price](https://tinybeans.helpscoutdocs.com/article/22-how-much-is-a-tinybeans-premium-subscription)
- FamilyAlbum Premium gives free photobook shipping, but shipping is still charged on prints, albums and pocket pages. — [FamilyAlbum help](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404344217113-What-s-the-difference-between-FamilyAlbum-Premium-and-the-free-version-What-features-are-included-in-each-service)
- Plum Print has no membership; digital files and gallery come free with a book. — [Plum Print pricing](https://www.plumprint.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- Bundled physical products are framed as **credits that expire** (Artkive) or **shipping waivers** (Tinybeans, FamilyAlbum), not as a guaranteed object arriving on a schedule. "A small keepsake made real every month or season" would be new in this category.

### Gaps
- Qeepsake Premium may include books; this was not verified.
- No data on how many Artkive members actually redeem the free book.

## 3. Cancellation, auto-renew and what happens to stored art (hostage-data risk)

### Takeaway
Hostage-data anger is the category's biggest reputational scar. Artkive's 2017 switch from a one-off app to membership produced "holding my kids art hostage" reviews and a $49 bulk-download fee it later dropped. Tinybeans is the best practice found: it never deletes on cancel and drops to the free 5GB view, but bulk export is paywalled and 12 months of inactivity can trigger deletion. Lifecake's closure shows the "the service dies" risk. In the UK, the DMCC Act subscription regime (reminder notices, cooling-off) is expected around Jan–spring 2027.

### Cited Findings
- Artkive App Store reviews (US), 2017:
  - The app moved to a required membership ("at a minimum of 3 dollars a month") after people had paid $4.99.
  - Reviewers said they would be locked out "unless I pay a fee" and called it "holding my kids art hostage".
  - A $49–$50 bulk-download option was criticised and then removed by the developer (15 May 2017).
  - Individual downloads took about 2 minutes per file.
  - The developer gave users about a month to download for free, and offered credits and a hardship fund.
  - Source: [Artkive US App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/artkive-save-kids-art/id549980508?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)
- Artkive in 2020: the app was free only up to 40 images, then "you must pay in 30 days or loose your photos". In 2023 a reviewer paid $40 for the box, $70 to finalise a book and $29 shipping, and found errors in the book. — [Artkive US App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/artkive-save-kids-art/id549980508?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)
- Artkive in the UK: a 2021 UK review says "No longer free so don't bother". — [Artkive UK App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/artkive-save-kids-art/id549980508)
- To cancel Artkive, you email help@artkiveapp.com or text a number; no self-serve cancel was described. Non-member images can be removed 90 days after notice (search snippet). — [Artkive FAQ: membership plans](https://faq.artkiveapp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038276252-All-About-Membership-Plans)
- Tinybeans:
  - "We'll never delete your memories just because you cancel... your newest photos (within the 5GB limit) will remain visible". Everything is kept should you resubscribe.
  - Cancel in-app via More > Manage Subscription; a web purchase is cancelled on the web billing page.
  - Bulk export is a Tinybeans+ feature.
  - An account inactive for 12 months may be permanently deleted, with email reminders first.
  - Sources: [Tinybeans: will my memories be deleted](https://tinybeans.helpscoutdocs.com/article/87-will-my-memories-be-deleted-if-i-cancel-my-subscription-or-downgrade); [Tinybeans: how to cancel](https://tinybeans.helpscoutdocs.com/article/26-explore-can-i-cancel-my-subscription); [Tinybeans: what if I stop using it](https://tinybeans.helpscoutdocs.com/article/88-what-happens-if-i-stop-using-tinybeans)
- App Store standard terms (Keepy, Tinybeans, Sago, Pok Pok, DUPLO, 23snaps): auto-renew unless turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends, managed in Apple account settings. Pok Pok notes that deleting the app does not cancel. Sago says no cancellation fees. — [Keepy UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/keepy-artwork-schoolwork/id647088205); [Pok Pok UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1550204730); [Sago UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/sago-mini-world-kids-games/id874425722)
- Lifecake closed "with little warning"; users were moved to BackThen, the migration failed for some, and paid storage was not refunded. — [BackThen UK App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1505173822)
- Keepy: no public statement found on what happens to keepies over the free cap after cancelling. — [appshunter Keepy cancel page](https://appshunter.io/ios/app/keepy-artwork-schoolwork/id647088205/how-to-cancel-subscription)
- LEGO DUPLO World (UK, 2024 review): the move from buying worlds to a subscription upset a parent who had bought 16 games ("charged twice"). — [LEGO DUPLO World UK App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/lego-duplo-world/id1458749093)
- UK DMCC Act subscription regime:
  - Advance reminder notices before renewal must state the date and amount. Missing one can give a right to cancel. There is a 14-day initial cooling-off period, plus renewal cooling-off in some cases.
  - Start date has slipped from spring 2026 to autumn 2026 to spring 2027. A DWF note of 22 Oct 2026 reports January 2027, not confirmed against an official source. Secondary legislation was still unpublished.
  - Sources: [DWF Oct 2026](https://dwfgroup.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2026/10/dmcca-subscription-contracts-regime-preparing-for-the-new-restrictions); [DWF Mar 2026](https://dwfgroup.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2026/3/subscription-contracts-preparing-for-change-in-2026); [CMS](https://cms.law/en/gbr/legal-updates/cancel-culture-uk-subscription-contracts-regime-takes-shape); [Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/uk-subscription-law-shakeup-new-rules-pushed-to-autumn-2026); [Taylor Wessing Apr 2026](https://www.taylorwessing.com/de/insights-and-events/insights/2026/04/subscription-contracts)

### Inferences
- The safest trust position, which fits "originals are sacred", is to **never lock or delete kept keepsakes because a subscription lapses**. Limit new perks instead, and keep free download and export.
- Turning a free thing into a paid thing is what triggers the backlash (Artkive 2017, DUPLO 2024). Launching with a clear free/paid line from day one avoids it.
- Self-serve web cancel plus DMCC-style reminders before the regime arrives would set Snickerdoodle apart from Artkive, which cancels only by email or text.

### Gaps
- No Keepy, FamilyAlbum or 23snaps policy found on data after a lapse.
- No Artkive policy found for *lapsed members*, as opposed to never-members.
- No Reddit threads were retrieved. Search returned App Store and blog content instead.

## 4. Review sentiment: praise and complaints

### Takeaway
Praise centres on decluttering without guilt, grandparents' access, and "done for you" convenience (Artkive Box). Complaints centre on surprise second invoices and vague pricing for mail-in services, high shipping, membership switches and hostage data, upload bugs and lost drawings, and slow box turnaround with poor communication. UK Mumsnet threads show the default UK behaviour is DIY: photograph, bin, keep a few, and maybe a photobook.

### Cited Findings
- Artkive Trustpilot:
  - Search snippets show about 320 reviews, summarised by Trustpilot as "overwhelmingly positive".
  - Complaints include cards that were lost with no tracking number, vague pricing ("Cost of photography/editing, books... not included"), and the two-payment model (box fee, then a per-piece charge).
  - The rate of replies to negative reviews conflicts between regional pages (93% vs 100%).
  - Sources: [Trustpilot UK Artkive](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/artkiveapp.com); [Trustpilot Artkive p2](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/artkiveapp.com?page=2)
  - A ThingTesting review complains of no updates on a daughter's artwork. That page also cites about 25 BBB complaints (not verified). — [ThingTesting](https://thingtesting.com/brands/cards-by-artkive/reviews)
- Artkive praise: "does its very tight niche well"; "steadfast and secure"; art tagged by age, date and child. — [Artkive US App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/artkive-save-kids-art/id549980508?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)
- Keepy UK:
  - Praise: declutters while keeping the memories, and grandparents enjoy the comments.
  - Complaints: in 2018, after a year of paying, drawings disappeared and crops did not save; in 2024 the app froze creating a child profile.
  - Source: [Keepy UK App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/keepy-artwork-schoolwork/id647088205)

### Inferences
- UK parents value the *ritual of choosing* a few favourites ("top 10 a year"). A membership built around a small curated keepsake, rather than "store everything", fits UK behaviour.
- Pricing transparency up front (no second invoice) is a cheap point of difference against mail-in services.

### Gaps
- Most visible App Store reviews are old (2013–2021). Current sentiment may differ.
- No UK Trustpilot data was gathered for Doodle Nest, Silk Giraffe or Cards to Books.

## 5. Known business numbers (subscribers, revenue, churn, conversion)

### Takeaway
Tinybeans is the only competitor with audited public numbers: about US$3.3M ARR in FY25, and about 90–95k paid subscribers including Qeepsake. Artkive's revenue (~$6M) is only a third-party estimate. No conversion or churn rates were found.

### Cited Findings
- Tinybeans FY25 (year to 30 Jun 2025):
  - Subscription revenue up 12% to US$3.32M, 69% of revenue; total revenue down 11% to US$4.82M as it moved away from ads.

- Even the category leader in family memory subscriptions makes only about US$3.3M a year from subscriptions. Subscription is a modest, retention-oriented revenue line in this space, not the main engine. Product sales still dominate for Artkive and Plum Print.

## 6. Gaps in the market (what none of them do)

### Takeaway
Gift subscriptions exist (23snaps, Tinybeans, Sago), but no kids' art service was found offering a **grandparent-bought keepsake membership** that delivers physical product. No one was found doing **auto-made yearly or birthday art books** from the year's drawings with one-tap approval, **monthly sticker sheets or mini-prints from this month's drawings**, or a **UK-made, UK-data-resident** art archive with a membership.

### Cited Findings
- Gift subscriptions found:
  - 23snaps sells 1/3/6/12-month Premium gifts (£7.49 to £52.99). — [23snaps UK App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id526481189)
  - Tinybeans has a "gift subscription ends" help article. — [Tinybeans gift subscription](https://tinybeans.helpscoutdocs.com/article/100-what-happens-when-my-tinybeans-gift-subscription-ends)
  - Sago Mini sells gift subscriptions. — [Sago Mini gifting](https://store.sagomini.com/gifting)
  - All three are digital-only gifts.
- The only auto-printed books found are Notabli's "Book subscriptions" (photos, US). — [Notabli App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/notabli/id580644870)
- FamilyAlbum's model of one subscriber unlocking Premium for the whole album shows that a payer who isn't the parent (for example a grandparent) can be accommodated. — [FamilyAlbum help: Premium Family / Pro](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038264494-What-is-FamilyAlbum-Premium)
- UK art-book services are all one-off, per-book purchases (see section 1).

### Inferences
- These openings look uncontested for a UK player:
  - a grandparent gift membership with a physical "made real" moment each season
  - an auto-assembled "year of drawings" book proposed on the birthday or at the end of the school year
  - sticker sheets from recent drawings
  - a promise that art is never held hostage, plus free export
- Apple Kids Category rules (parental gate, no third-party analytics) favour selling the membership on the web, not inside a kids' draw app.

### Gaps
- Sticker-sheet or monthly-print subscriptions from kids' art were not searched exhaustively. Etsy-style sellers may exist.
- Chatbooks and Shutterfly kids' art book offers were not researched.
