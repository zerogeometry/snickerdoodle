# UK-wide paid email/newsletter and website placements reaching parents and grandparents (cheapest per 1,000 reached, live 26 Nov to 6 Dec 2026)

Research date: 18 Nov 2026. Public pages only. Nothing was contacted, requested or booked.
Key: **S** = published figure (URL given). **E** = my estimate (method stated). "Page date not shown" means the page carried no date when I read it on 18 Nov 2026, so the figure is current only as of that read.
Default open-rate assumption where none is published: 30% (E). VAT: prices shown "+VAT" are also given inc-VAT (x1.2), in case Snickerdoodle can't reclaim VAT.

**On-target share (added brief):** this is the estimated share of a list's readers who are parents of children aged about 0 to 10, or grandparents. Rankings use **cost per 1,000 on-target opens** = price ÷ (subscribers × open rate × on-target share) × 1,000.

## Which UK-wide parent/family/grandparent newsletters or sites publish a rate card under ~£300 for a single send or feature (list size, open rate, audience, price, how to book, creative)?

### Takeaway
Only one genuinely UK-wide parent list publishes both a price under £300 and a list size: the **Picniq weekly newsletter feature at £150+VAT to 140,000+ subscribers**, a UK family days-out site. That works out at about £1.07 per 1,000 subscribers and roughly £4.80 per 1,000 on-target opens (E). Most other national parent and grandparent brands (Day Out With The Kids, Kidadl, Families Online, Emma's Diary, Gransnet, Rest Less/Silversurfers, The Motherload) publish no price at all. The other cheap published rates belong to regional lists (London, Essex) or to gift-guide slots whose reach is not published.

- At about £12 profit an order, Picniq at £180 inc VAT breaks even at **15 orders** from ~31,500 on-target opens (≈1 order per 2,100 on-target opens). London Mums (£75) needs about **6 orders**. Both are tough but not absurd; Picniq gives by far the most reach per pound.
- Picniq's per-subscriber price (~£1.07 per 1,000) is far below the $10 to $45 market benchmarks. That suggests either a feature is one of several items in a send, or the list is less engaged. Ask what "featured" means (position, how many features per send) before paying.
- A Picniq newsletter is read by people planning **days out**, not shopping for gifts. Lead with "a gift made from their drawing, made in the UK, order by 14 Dec" rather than a product list.
- Happity (140k+) and DOWTK (>30% opens) are the two unpublished-price lists most likely to beat Picniq on on-target CPM if quoted at or below ~£200. They're worth a single price question each from the founder (not by me).

## Which are realistically still bookable for a late-Nov/early-Dec send (lead times)?

### Takeaway
None of the publishers checked publishes lead times or an availability calendar. Picniq's weekly newsletter is the most plausible to still have a slot in the 26 Nov to 6 Dec window (weekly cadence, small-advertiser rate card, email booking), but this is unconfirmed. Given the 14 Dec last order date, a send after about 3 Dec leaves little time to convert.

### Cited Findings
- Picniq sends weekly newsletters and books by email; no lead time stated (S) — [Picniq advertise](https://www.picniq.co.uk/about/advertise)
- Essex Mums offers online "book & pay" for newsletter ads, implying short lead times, but it is regional (S) — [Essex Mums book & pay](https://www.essexmums.com/blog/book-pay-for-ads-online/)
- DOWTK routes enquiries through a sales funnel ("Get Started"), with no availability shown (S) — [DOWTK marketing packages](https://www.dayoutwiththekids.co.uk/marketing-packages-and-listings)
- Big group publishers (Mumsnet £10k minimum, Family Traveller £3.5k to £5k/month) are out of budget whatever the lead time — [Mumsnet Advertising](https://advertising.mumsnet.com/); [Family Traveller media pack](https://mediapack.familytraveller.com/)

### Inferences
- Realistic in time and under £300: **Picniq (best)**, London Mums, Get Blogged slots, The London Mother (if still trading), Working Families small insert. Small weekly or fortnightly lists can usually fit a sponsor within 1 to 2 weeks (E), but an early-December slot is the busiest time of year for gift advertisers. **The founder would need to ask by about 20 to 22 Nov.**
- Target a send of **26 Nov to 2 Dec** to leave 12+ days before the 14 Dec framed-print cutoff. A Black Friday/Cyber Monday-week send (27 Nov to 1 Dec) competes with heavy retail email; 2 to 4 Dec may get better attention but a shorter buying window (E).

### Gaps
- No publisher states its lead time or remaining December inventory; this can only be confirmed by asking, which was out of scope.

## Ranked top 5 by cost per 1,000 on-target opens (bookable in time, under ~£300)

### Takeaway
1. **Picniq weekly newsletter feature**: £150+VAT, 140k+ UK-wide parents. ~£4.76 per 1,000 on-target opens (£5.71 inc VAT), assuming retail is accepted. Clear first choice.
2. **London Mums newsletter**: £75, 41k. ~£10.16 per 1,000 on-target opens, but London-centred, not UK-wide.
3. **Get Blogged gift-guide slots**: £20.80 to £26 each. Cheapest cash and UK-wide via blogs, but reach is unpublished, so CPM can't be computed. Buy only on blogs showing parent audiences and real traffic.
4. **Essex Mums newsletter advertorial**: £45+VAT. ~£21 per 1,000 on-target opens (E), regional. A cheap, self-serve test only.
5. **Working Families small newsletter insert**: £250 (Nov 2025 card). UK-wide working parents, but list size is unknown and the audience is mixed with employers, so it's the weakest on value per pound. Include only if the founder can learn the list size.

Watch-list (ask the price; could rank #1 or #2 if quoted ≤£200): **Happity What's On (140k+ parents)** and **Day Out With The Kids email (>30% opens)**. No grandparent list was found with a published price under £300.

### Cited Findings
- See figures and sources above: [Picniq advertise](https://www.picniq.co.uk/about/advertise); [Essex Mums book & pay](https://www.essexmums.com/blog/book-pay-for-ads-online/); [Working Families Rate Card (Nov 2025)](https://workingfamilies.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Working-Families-Sponsorship-Rate-Card.pdf); [DOWTK marketing packages](https://www.dayoutwiththekids.co.uk/marketing-packages-and-listings)

### Inferences
- With a ~£500 total budget: Picniq (£180 inc VAT) plus 2 or 3 Get Blogged slots (~£75) plus London Mums (£75) costs about £330, leaving room for one of the watch-list lists if quoted low (E).

### Gaps
- The ranking rests on assumed open rates (30%) and assumed on-target shares for every list. No list publishes a child-age or grandparent breakdown.
