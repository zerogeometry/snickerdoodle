# UK parent and family LIFESTYLE newsletters bookable for a 26 Nov to 4 Dec 2026 send at about £300 or less

Research date: 18 Nov 2026. Public pages only; nobody contacted. S = published figure (URL given). E = my estimate.
Default maths: opens = 30% of list (E). "On-target" = share of readers who are UK parents of under-11s or grandparents (E, stated per row).
Personal names and personal email addresses seen on publisher pages are deliberately left out. Where a page gives only a named person, I say "named contact on page".

## Which UK family LIFESTYLE newsletters publish a price and list size, and what do they cost per 1,000 on-target opens?

### Takeaway
No UK-wide parent **lifestyle** newsletter was found with both a published price under £300 and a list size. The only lifestyle-led list with both is **London Mums (£75, 41k, London-centred)**, found earlier. Every national lifestyle brand checked this round either publishes no price (The Green Parent, The Parent Edit, The Motherload, MadeForMums, Mother&Baby, Peanut), has no visible newsletter ad product (JUNO), or looks defunct online (Junior). Market benchmarks suggest a 15k to 50k lifestyle list would normally quote well above £300, so Picniq's £150 for 140k+ stays a clear price outlier.

- **Break-even quotes to match London Mums (£10.16 per 1,000 on-target opens):** The Green Parent would need to cost about **£27** for one send (15,000 × 30% × 55% ≈ 2,475 on-target opens). Lemon-Aid would need about **£89**. To match Picniq (£4.76), they would need about £12 and £42. On a published-rate basis, none of the lifestyle lists is likely to beat Picniq.
- On the market benchmarks above, a 15k to 50k lifestyle list typically quotes about £400 to £2,400 (E, converting $500 to $3,000), which is above budget. Expect most "worth one email" replies to come back over £300 unless the founder asks for a small gift-guide line or a remnant slot.
- At about £12 profit an order, London Mums (£75) breaks even at about 6 to 7 orders. A £150 lifestyle send to 15k (Green Parent) would need about 13 orders from about 2,500 on-target opens, roughly 1 in 190. That is unrealistic for a £60 to £65 product.
- **Fit vs reach:** The Parent Edit has the best content fit seen (its 2026 guides already include a "one-off personalised Christmas gift" page and age-banded children's gift guides). But it publishes no reach figures, so it can't be ranked on cost.
- Editorial gift guides are normally written by October, so for a 26 Nov to 4 Dec send, only **paid** advertorial or newsletter slots are realistic. Ask by about 20 to 22 Nov.

## Ranked top 5 lifestyle newsletters by cost per 1,000 on-target opens, and what should replace Picniq

### Takeaway
**Published price under ~£300 (lifestyle only):**
1. **London Mums newsletter**: £75, 41k. About **£10.16 per 1,000 on-target opens (E)**. Lifestyle-led, but London-centred.
2. **Essex Mums newsletter advertorial**: £45+VAT, ~10k (old figure). About **£21.40 (E)**. Regional; a cheap test only.
3. **Working Families small insert**: £250 (Nov 2025 card). Working-parent audience, list size unpublished, so not computable. Rank it only if the list size turns out to be 50k or more.

That is all there is. No other UK parent lifestyle list publishes a price under £300.

**Price unpublished but worth one email (in order):**
1. **The Parent Edit**: best product fit (live 2026 personalised-gift and age-banded children's guides; advertorial gift guides offered). Ask for list size, open rate and a single newsletter or gift-guide line price. Use the /contact form.
2. **The Green Parent**: 15k weekly newsletter (2024) plus a Christmas Gift Guide supplement. Only good value at about £30 or less per send; ask for a gift-guide or newsletter line rate. Use the advertise-page form.
3. **The Motherload**: 95k Facebook community and 60k monthly users. Ask whether it has a newsletter and its size.
4. **Lemon-Aid (Reach)**: ~45k parents (2022). Worth it only if it still runs and Reach will sell a single slot under about £90.
5. **MadeForMums / Mother&Baby**: biggest lifestyle reach, but group publishers usually carry minimums like Mumsnet's and Netmums'. Ask once only if the founder wants a definitive "no".

**Replacing Picniq:** No lifestyle list matches Picniq's reach for the money. If Picniq feels too attraction-centric, the like-for-like swap is **London Mums (£75)**. It is lifestyle-led and its CPM is the best published after Picniq's (about 2x Picniq's cost per on-target open), but UK-wide reach drops from 140k to 41k, mostly London. Pair it with one price question to **The Parent Edit**, the strongest lifestyle gift-guide fit nationally. My recommendation on the numbers is still to keep Picniq as the UK-wide send. Lead the creative with gifts rather than days out, and use London Mums as the lifestyle complement rather than the replacement.
