# Bristol & South West digital ad placements a tiny gift shop can still book (run window ~25 Nov to 12 Dec 2026)

Research date: 18 Nov 2026. Read-only: public pages and PDFs only. Nothing contacted, no forms, no sign-ups. Facebook groups and Instagram profiles were not checked (need sign-in). Some sites (bristolparent.co.uk) showed a bot check and were not read. Bristol24/7 blocks the fetch tool, so its pages were read with a plain page request instead.

Break-even maths used below (inference): margin is about £5 an order, so a placement needs about **price ÷ 5 orders** to pay back. £45 = 9 orders; £150 = 30; £175 = 35; £800 = 160.

## Q1. Audience size and fit with local parents and grandparents (ranked shortlist)

### Takeaway
Nothing found is a bookable *digital* channel aimed only at Bristol parents of young children with a published price. The best options are a cheap general-lifestyle newsletter in affluent north/west Bristol (The Bristol Magazine, £45), a hyperlocal self-serve test (Nextdoor Local Deals, "from £1"), and the big general Bristol channel (Bristol24/7: newsletter, Instagram, a sponsored gift guide that ran on 27 Nov last year). Family-specific sites (Bristol Parent, Bristol Mum, Practically Perfect Mums, Primary Times digital) take ads, but none publishes prices or audience figures. You would have to ask each one, so they can't be judged from public information.

### Ranked shortlist (likely sales per £ × can it still run by early Dec)
1. **The Bristol Magazine – "Weekend E-dition" newsletter banner or product box** – £45 per send, £150 for 4 (ex VAT). 5,000+ subscribers (figure dated July 2022/2024). Readers are affluent BS8/BS9/BS6, so many are grandparents. It covers Westbury-on-Trym, Stoke Bishop, Henleaze and Sneyd Park, next to BS11. The cheapest published per-send price found, and needs only 9 orders to break even. Confirmed on their own 2026 pack.
2. **Nextdoor Local Deals / Ads Manager (self-serve)** – "Deals starting from £1", aimed at chosen neighbourhoods or a whole city. You can stop it any time and spend as little as you like. It is the best way to test Shirehampton, Sea Mills, Westbury, Henleaze and Portishead. Price is confirmed on Nextdoor's UK page. No minimum or speed is published.
3. **Bristol24/7 – sponsored story in the daily newsletter, or a slot in a sponsored Christmas Gift Guide** – big general Bristol audience. The 2022 rates were £175 for a newsletter story and £350 for 4 days of banner. Instagram has 100k+ followers. The 2025 Gift Guide was a sponsored list of indie businesses, published Thu 27 Nov 2025. No 2026 guide was on their gift-guide index on 18 Nov 2026, which suggests a late-Nov guide may still be sellable (inference). The audience is all of Bristol, not parents, and you need about 35 orders at £175 to break even, so it is riskier.
4. **The Bath Magazine – Weekend Edition newsletter** – £45 per week, £135 for 4 weeks (2024 pack). About 5,000 subscribers (2022). Affluent Bath readers. Same publisher as no. 1 (MC Publishing). The 2026 rate is not confirmed.
5. **Family sites with ads "on request"**: Bristol Parent (bristolparent.co.uk), The Bath & Wiltshire Parent, Bristol Mum (sidebar banners and sponsored posts), Practically Perfect Mums (Portishead / North Somerset, running since 2012). These are the best fit (parents), but none publishes a price, audience figure or deadline. Small owner-run sites can often post within days (guess).
6. **Primary Times (Avon) – digital** (website MPU/leaderboard, "tailored e-shots") – price on application for Avon. Cardiff's 2026 pack shows £250/month for a leaderboard or MPU, a useful guide to the likely price. Very good fit (families of 4–11s across Bristol, Bath, North Somerset and South Glos), but the e-shot list size isn't published.

8. **Visit Bristol / Visit West "Merry Bristmas" Christmas campaign (2026 rate card)** – 23,000+ newsletter subscribers at about 36% opens, 100k+ Instagram, 72,298 campaign-page views (Nov–Dec 2025). The cheapest package (Bronze) is £800 + VAT for non-members, and it gives a Visit *Bath* newsletter banner, not Bristol. The audience is visitors, and the slots are limited and probably sold. Poor value at this budget.
9. **Bristol24/7 Better Business membership** – £215/month or £2,500/year on the live join page. It gives up to one member feature a month and 20% off ads. Too expensive for this budget.
10. **Not useful for this**: The Bristol Cable (2025 ad pack shows a quarterly print paper only, sold through EMSM, with no digital product). Mumbler (no Bristol franchise; the nearest is Bournemouth, Christchurch and Poole). Hoop (sells only to activity and event organisers, no gift-shop product found). Global AdPower / DAX and Heart Bristol (self-serve exists, no minimum found; audio is a poor fit at this budget). Community mags in Shirehampton, Henleaze/Westbury and BS9 (print only; no e-newsletter ad product found).

### Inferences
- **Best sales per £ (inference):** The Bristol Magazine newsletter at £45 needs about 9 orders. It is the only published, cheap, quick-to-run newsletter slot in Snickerdoodle's own corner of Bristol (BS9/BS8 sit next to BS11). Grandparents in that readership fit "photo/drawing keepsake for the grandchildren" well. A 4-send run (£150) from about 27 Nov to 18 Dec covers the cut-off dates but needs 30 orders to pay back. Book 1 or 2 sends first (around 27 Nov and 4 Dec).
- **Nextdoor** is the lowest-risk test: no minimum published, self-serve, you can stop it any time, and you can aim it at BS11/BS9/Portishead neighbourhoods, where Nextdoor users skew towards homeowners and older adults (a general impression, not sourced). Pair it with a partner/discount code to measure results.
- **Bristol24/7** has the largest reach but no parent targeting. A £175 newsletter story needs about 35 orders. If 12k+ subscribers at about 33% opens still holds, that's about 4,000 opens. At a guessed 1–2% click rate that's about 40–80 clicks, too few for 35 orders at normal conversion rates. **Don't book unless** they offer a cheap gift-guide slot (a guess of about £100–£200; no price published) or take a commission or code deal instead.
- The Visit West Bronze package (£800 + VAT non-member) needs about 160 orders and gives a *Bath* newsletter banner to a visitor audience. Skip it.
- A Primary Times Avon e-shot or MPU is the best *fit* digitally (families of 4–11s), but price is on application and probably £250+ (Cardiff gives a guide), and the Winter print issue (published 24 Nov 2025 last year) is already covered elsewhere. Only worth asking about if a digital add-on can be bundled with the existing print booking.
